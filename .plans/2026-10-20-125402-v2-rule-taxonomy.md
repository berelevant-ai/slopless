## Type Decisions

- **Empty takeaways** (`empty-takeaway`) belong under semantic thinness.
  - "That is the real promise." and "The lesson was simple." express the same defect: labeling a takeaway without adding useful content.
  - Consolidate this behavior from `deictic-summary`, `generic-lesson-extraction`, `lesson-framing`, and the relevant parts of `generic-signposting` and `affirmation-closers` into one evaluation type.
- **Vague attribution** (`vague-attribution`) belongs under unsupported authority.
  - "Experts agree ..." and "Research shows ..." imply support while leaving the relevant source unspecified.
  - Consolidate that behavior from `authority-padding`, `uncited-authority`, and `weasel-attribution`. Preserve relevant named sources and meaningful credentials as no-hits.
- **Empty content and unnecessary introductions have different tests.**
  - "The lesson was simple." supplies an empty takeaway.
  - "The lesson was simple: stop retries after three failures." supplies a concrete instruction inside an unnecessary introductory frame.
  - The first belongs to semantic thinness; the second can trigger boilerplate while remaining a no-hit for empty takeaways.
- **Puffery judges inflated evaluation; unsupported authority judges how support is presented.**
  - "A world-class breakthrough" is puffery.
  - "Leading experts agree" invokes unspecified authority. A passage can contain both defects and receive both labels.
- **Use wordiness instead of concision.** The question is whether words can be removed or shortened while preserving the intended meaning.
- Generate development and holdout sets per consolidated issue type. Treat equivalent wordings as variants; use separate labels when a passage has different defects.

## Required Input

- Each finalized issue definition declares `input: passage` or `input: text` alongside its family and type. This describes the context needed to judge the issue.
  - `input: passage` uses a self-contained excerpt with enough surrounding sentences, paragraphs, or headings to judge the construction.
  - `input: text` uses the complete article, story, or reply in its original order. A short complete reply still counts as a text.
- Assign input per issue type. Different types in the same family can require different inputs; define separate types when local and whole-text judgments assess different defects.
- Apply these assignments when defining the identified types:
  - Empty takeaway: `input: passage`.
  - Negation reframe, including cross-sentence constructions: `input: passage`.
  - Heading/body restatement: `input: passage`, including the heading and opening together.
  - Buried answer: `input: text`, judged from the question, main point, and organization expressed in the text itself.
  - Repeated explanations within the submitted text: `input: text`.
  - Repeated closers across sections: `input: text`.
- Density reporting can count passage-level detections across a text. Keep that aggregation separate from the detector's required input.
- Keep complete evaluation examples for text-level checks in `datasets/*/texts/`; use `datasets/*/passages/` for independent excerpts. Preserve their original context when creating development and holdout splits.
- These are V2 definition requirements. The legacy coverage list below remains an inventory while consolidated types and their input declarations are completed.

## Consolidated Semantic-Thinness Types

Semantic thinness is a missing substantive contribution: the wording presents a conclusion, development, explanation, or relationship while leaving its content unspecified. These nine definitions replace overlapping legacy names when building this family's V2 datasets and prompts. Production behavior remains unchanged.

### Shared Decisions

- All nine types use `input: passage`. Include the surrounding sentences or paragraphs needed to resolve a reference or supplied explanation.
- Judge the complete passage before labeling a phrase. A heading, adjacent sentence, or concrete example can supply the information that the phrase leaves implicit.
- Preserve an implicit point when the passage makes it unambiguous. The information can be inferred from the writing rather than stated in a prescribed sentence form.
- Names, numbers, technical terms, and words such as "because" protect a claim only when they supply its missing content. Unrelated detail leaves an empty claim intact.
- Use the most specific type for one defect. An empty takeaway receives that label rather than additional labels for significance or abstraction. Label separate defects separately.
- Preserve useful compression, conventional technical terms, and figurative descriptions whose meaning is explained by the passage. Judge the contribution rather than a particular word, sentence length, or writing genre.
- The source identifiers under each type describe behavior to consolidate. Split mixed legacy checks by their actual construction; each identifier can contribute different branches to different types or families.

### Empty Takeaway (`empty-takeaway`)

- **Input:** `passage`.
- **Catch:** A lesson, answer, recommendation, or concluding point is announced or graded while the passage supplies only a generic virtue or evaluation instead of the point itself.
  - Hit: "The lesson was simple. The answer was consistency." Snippets: "The lesson was simple" and "The answer was consistency".
  - No-hit: "The lesson was simple: reject expired tokens before refreshing the session."
- **Boundary:** A concrete instruction inside an unnecessary introductory frame belongs to boilerplate and signposting. A useful stated takeaway remains a no-hit for this type.
- **Legacy sources:** `deictic-summary`, `generic-lesson-extraction`, `point-is-frame`, `lesson-framing`, `affirmation-closers`, `real-work-begins`, and the empty-takeaway branches of `generic-signposting`, `contrastive-aphorism`, `empty-emphasis`, and `vague-connective-payoff`.

### Unspecified Change (`unspecified-change`)

- **Input:** `passage`.
- **Catch:** A change, transition, improvement, worsening, or shift in difficulty is announced while the passage leaves what changed unspecified.
  - Hit: "Something had shifted. The work felt different now." Snippets: "Something had shifted" and "The work felt different now".
  - No-hit: "The review changed from a weekly meeting to written comments on each release."
- **Boundary:** The surrounding passage can supply the change. When the single omission concerns coverage or constraints, use unspecified scope, including "the problem is narrower now". Missing cost or risk consequences belong to hollow significance; stock atmospheric scene changes belong to narrative craft.
- **Legacy sources:** `empty-scene-transition`, `something-shifted`, `vague-threshold-change`, and the change-announcement branches of `hollow-significance`, `contrastive-aphorism`, `deictic-summary`, and `empty-emphasis`.

### Hollow Significance (`hollow-significance`)

- **Input:** `passage`.
- **Catch:** Importance, impact, benefit, risk, cost, or measurement is invoked as the payoff while the passage leaves the practical consequence or measured result unspecified.
  - Hit: "The distinction matters. The report put a number on it." Snippets: "The distinction matters" and "The report put a number on it".
  - No-hit: "The distinction determines whether the tenant or landlord pays for the repair."
  - No-hit for measurement: "The report measured the average delay at eighteen hours."
- **Boundary:** A qualitative consequence can resolve a significance claim. A measurement claim needs an interpretable metric, value, scale, or calculation; a clear consequence alone leaves an unrelated empty measurement claim intact.
  - Inflated evaluation belongs to puffery; describing fear or tension as atmosphere belongs to narrative craft.
- **Legacy sources:** The significance branches of `hollow-significance`, `empty-significance-occasion`, `empty-quantification`, `vague-summary-cost`, `generic-pressure-or-stakes`, `empty-emphasis`, `demonstrative-emphasis`, `superficial-analysis`, `observer-guidance`, and `vague-connective-payoff`.
  - The broad significance matcher and the importance and abstract-cost branches of `generic-signposting` contribute here when the consequence is missing.

### Generic Realization (`generic-realization`)

- **Input:** `passage`.
- **Catch:** Understanding, discovery, recognition, or revelation is announced while what was learned remains unspecified.
  - Hit: "She finally understood. The truth had revealed itself." Snippets: "She finally understood" and "The truth had revealed itself".
  - No-hit: "She finally understood: the router still used the old DNS record."
- **Boundary:** The insight can be stated before or after the announcement. Naming an invoice, room, or other detail alone does not reveal what someone learned from it.
- **Legacy sources:** `generic-realization` and the discovery or revelation branches of `truth-answer-moves` and `deictic-summary`.

### Circular Explanation (`circular-explanation`)

- **Input:** `passage`.
- **Catch:** An apparent reason or explanation restates the original abstraction, including paraphrases, while adding no explanatory step.
  - Hit: "This work matters because it helps us focus on the work that matters." Snippet: "matters because it helps us focus on the work that matters".
  - No-hit: "This work matters because it restores customer records after a disk failure."
- **Boundary:** Repeated vocabulary can describe a real mechanism. Repeating an earlier point elsewhere in the text belongs to wordiness and redundancy; the defect here is the explanation itself.
- **Legacy sources:** The circular branches of `recursive-meaning-frame` and `body-knows`, plus circular explanatory branches within broader signposting patterns.

### Unexplained Metaphor (`unexplained-metaphor`)

- **Input:** `passage`.
- **Catch:** A metaphor, personified abstraction, or sweeping equivalence substitutes for the relationship the claim is supposed to explain, and the passage leaves that relationship unclear.
  - Hit: "The strategy wanted to become more honest with itself." Snippet: "wanted to become more honest with itself".
  - No-hit: "The signing key is the lock on the release: only builds signed with it can be installed."
- **Boundary:** Preserve literal uses and figurative explanations with an identifiable relationship. Stock physicalized emotion and atmosphere remain in narrative craft; an empty announcement of a specific insight belongs to generic realization.
- **Legacy sources:** `abstract-metaphor-claim`, `abstract-agency-personification`, `missing-conversation-plan`, and the figurative branches of `body-knows`, `truth-answer-moves`, `recursive-meaning-frame`, and `observer-guidance`.

### Unspecified Scope (`unspecified-scope`)

- **Input:** `passage`.
- **Catch:** A task, question, product, or remit is described as narrow, bounded, focused, or otherwise restricted while its actual coverage or constraints remain unspecified.
  - Hit: "The remit remains bounded. The problem is narrower now." Snippets: "The remit remains bounded" and "The problem is narrower now".
  - No-hit: "The remit remains bounded: it covers registration and login, while the payments team handles billing."
- **Boundary:** Stated inclusions, exclusions, or constraints can explain the scope without measurements. Assign this type rather than unspecified change when the single omission concerns a scope boundary. Preserve physical dimensions and established technical meanings, such as a bounded queue whose capacity is defined.
- **Legacy sources:** The restriction and scope branches of `generic-signposting`, including the narrow-frame matcher. A distinction expressed only as a different kind of job belongs to unspecified relationship.

### Unspecified Relationship (`unspecified-relationship`)

- **Input:** `passage`.
- **Catch:** Entities, activities, or alternatives are described as connected, associated, different, or equivalent while the relevant relationship or distinction remains unspecified.
  - Hit: "The audit and deployment are connected. That is a different kind of relationship." Snippets: "are connected" and "a different kind of relationship".
  - No-hit: "Deployment starts after the audit approves the release candidate."
- **Boundary:** Preserve a broad but informative relationship appropriate to the passage. Role names and concise interface descriptions can convey useful information; figurative substitutions are judged under unexplained metaphor.
- **Legacy sources:** `different-job-summary` and relationship-labeling branches of `generic-signposting`. This also incorporates Humanizer's vague-association behavior.
  - Preserve informative actor/component assignments. Assess removable wording under wordiness, excessive repeated syntax under rhythm, and additional disposable announcements under unnecessary framing.

### Missing Actor (`missing-actor`)

- **Input:** `passage`.
- **Catch:** The passage makes responsibility for an action or decision relevant, then obscures who performed it when that information is needed to understand the stated event or instruction.
  - Hit: "Either Legal or Finance may approve the release. Approval has been granted. Send the incident report to the approving department." Snippet: "Approval has been granted".
  - No-hit: "Legal approved the release. Approval was recorded in the launch checklist."
- **Boundary:** Preserve passive sentences, recoverable subjects, and instructions whose actor is irrelevant or already clear. Judge the missing responsibility from the passage itself.
- **Source:** Humanizer's missing-actor behavior supplies this addition; the legacy semantic-thinness family has no dedicated actor check.

## Consolidated Puffery And Overstatement Types

These two passage-level types judge the presentation of claims using the supplied writing. External verification of whether a claim is true belongs to the editor. Freeze these definitions before preparing the family's datasets.

### Inflated Evaluation (`inflated-evaluation`)

- **Input:** `passage`.
- **Catch:** Praise, condemnation, comparative superiority, importance, impact, or future success is expressed with grandiosity or certainty that the passage's concrete account does not justify.
  - Hit: "A minor menu update has redefined the entire discipline of teaching."
  - Hit: "Despite the difficulties ahead, our unwavering commitment ensures a triumphant future."
  - No-hit: "The update reduced the steps needed to record attendance from six to two. It is the quickest version in our timed classroom tests."
- **Boundary:** Judge the strength of the evaluation, including ordinary-sounding unsupported superiority; preserve modest personal preferences and proportionate qualitative judgments. A specific basis can support an evaluation without numbers. Named people, citations, dates, digits, or quotation marks alone do not settle whether the evaluation fits.
- **Boundary:** Preserve critical discussion of inflated wording, actual awards described with their scope, conditional forecasts with an explained basis, conventional technical terms, and literal nonevaluative statements. Literal wording remains eligible when it makes an unsupported evaluative claim. Attribution alone does not exempt promotional hyperbole adopted or repeated as an operative claim.
- **Legacy mapping:** Prestige, breakthrough, mastery, exceptional achievement, comparative judgment, and exaggerated benefit/harm branches of `puffery-evaluative-claim` contribute here. Its unsupported importance claims contribute here when inflated, or to hollow significance when the practical consequence alone is missing.
- **Legacy mapping:** Challenge-and-recovery assertions in `formulaic-challenges` contribute here when optimism exceeds the stated basis. Generic introductions belong to boilerplate and signposting; unnamed consequences or lessons belong to semantic thinness. Its digit and colon exclusions are retired as semantic criteria.
- **Legacy mapping:** The superlative-source branch of `universalizing-claims` contributes here when it inflates a conclusion about the greatest lessons or breakthroughs. An unspecified lesson alone belongs to empty takeaway. Figurative business-metric assertions contribute to unexplained metaphor when their missing relationship is the sole defect.

### Sweeping Generalization (`sweeping-generalization`)

- **Input:** `passage`.
- **Catch:** The prose presumes shared beliefs, desires, feelings, preferences, or habitual behavior across a broad human group, treating a varied population as if it speaks or acts with one voice without a meaningful boundary or supporting account.
  - Hit: "Every founder secretly wants the same kind of empire."
  - Hit: "We all know that choosing the expensive option is what serious people do."
  - No-hit: "Every founder at yesterday's workshop selected the monthly plan on the signed order form."
- **Boundary:** Preserve bounded observations about a defined group, attributed research scoped to its actual sample, meaningful tendencies with described conditions, individual opinions, invitations and genuine questions, procedural requirements, and ordinary factual or physiological statements. Naming a profession, place, or subgroup alone leaves unsupported uniform beliefs or motives eligible; an observed or meaningfully explained basis must support that uniformity. Broad quantifiers alone are insufficient.
- **Boundary:** A rhetorical question that presumes universal agreement remains eligible. A sample observation supports that sample rather than every human or an entire industry. Unrelated metrics, names, and qualifiers such as many do not repair a sweeping presumption.
- **Legacy mapping:** Human-group desires, certainty verbs, and habitual group-behavior branches of `universalizing-claims` contribute here. Its blanket digit/question exclusions are replaced by the meaning-based boundaries above; ordinary counts such as many reasons remain outside this type.
- **Family boundary:** Inflated claims about a product's greatness use inflated evaluation. Invoking unspecified experts as evidence belongs to unsupported authority. Separate defects can receive separate judgments in later mixed-set evaluation.

## Consolidated Unsupported-Authority Types

These two passage-level types judge the support presented in the submitted writing. Read the complete passage for source identification, relevant expertise, or a stated observation. External verification remains with the editor.

### Vague Attribution (`vague-attribution`)

- **Input:** `passage`.
- **Catch:** The writer invokes experts, research, evidence, consensus, or other people's judgment to support a claim while leaving the relevant source or account unspecified.
  - Hit: "Industry specialists agree that the new booking screen builds trust. We will present it at Friday's meeting."
  - No-hit: "In our usability sessions, six of eight participants found the cancellation button without help. These observations support keeping the new layout."
- **Boundary:** A contextual description can identify a particular source or supply the relevant observed basis without a formal citation or proper name. A profession, field, or broad setting alone leaves the attribution unspecified. Resolve references using the complete passage, including information supplied after the attribution. Preserve direct observations, relevant identifiable advice, and ordinary factual explanations that invoke no unspecified authority.
- **Boundary:** Preserve meaningful uncertainty while judging source identification separately; hedging an anonymous appeal leaves its attribution eligible. Digits, technical detail, dates, links, and named people protect only the attribution they identify or explain. A description of methods or results can provide the relevant account; an unrelated metric leaves an anonymous appeal intact.
- **Boundary:** Preserve clearly framed examination, rejection, or documentation of someone else's authority appeal. Ordinary attribution such as "Experts say X" remains eligible when unnamed authority supports the passage's operative claim; explicit agreement is unnecessary.
- **Family boundary:** An identified source whose unrelated prestige is used to establish a claim belongs to prestige padding. A substantive claim left unspecified belongs to semantic thinness; an excessive evaluation belongs to puffery. Judge separate defects separately in later mixed sets.
- **Legacy sources:** `authority-padding` evidence/research branches, `uncited-authority`, and `weasel-attribution`. Replace their sentence-local citation, named-organization, digit, and unquoted-only exemptions with the source-aware boundaries above.

### Prestige Padding (`prestige-padding`)

- **Input:** `passage`.
- **Catch:** Reputation, credentials, experience, famous associations, media attention, or follower counts are presented as support for a claim beyond what that status establishes in the passage.
  - Hit: "The adviser has two million followers, so her account proves the treatment is safe."
  - No-hit: "The adviser has two million followers; the campaign would put our notice in front of a large audience."
- **Boundary:** Preserve qualifications establishing relevant expertise or eligibility, first-hand experience supporting a bounded observation, and counts or media mentions describing reach or recognition. A credential need not be followed by a research paper when its stated role explains its relevance. Preserve biography and credits when they describe background, reach, or recognition; catch status lists used explicitly or implicitly to establish a claim beyond that relevance.
- **Boundary:** Judge the inference made from status. Relevant expertise can support advice within its stated scope. Catch status used as conclusive proof, or extended beyond the expertise, experience, reach, or recognition described. Preserve clearly framed examination, rejection, or documentation of an authority appeal, and a fictional character's limited belief unless the passage adopts it; ordinary attribution alone does not exempt an operative status-based claim.
- **Family boundary:** Exaggerated praise alone belongs to inflated evaluation. An unsupported anonymous expert appeal belongs to vague attribution. An unnecessary credential preface that still supplies relevant support can belong to boilerplate while remaining a no-hit here.
- **Legacy sources:** `humble-bragger`, the `authority-padding` prestige branch, and Humanizer's accepted prestige-list addition covering outlet names, follower counts, and credentials. Replace fixed phrase bans with relevance-based judgments.

## Consolidated Staged-Contrasts Types

One passage-level type consolidates thin rhetorical foils and gratuitous objections. Both require judging whether the opposition contributes useful meaning; their different wording does not require separate labels. Read the complete passage, including intervening sentences or paragraphs.

### Staged Opposition (`staged-opposition`)

- **Input:** `passage`.
- **Catch:** The passage stages a rejection, reversal, comparison, or rebuttal chiefly for rhetorical force while the opposition contributes little useful distinction, clarification, or narrative development. Include thin rhetorical foils and gratuitous objections, accusations, denials, or rejected proposals, whether explicit or implied.
  - Hit: "We are not delivering a schedule. We are delivering possibility."
  - No-hit: "The schedule records planned departures; the tracking page records the train's current position."
- **Boundary:** Cover single-clause, cross-sentence, and multi-paragraph versions; contractions and alternative negators; absence, missingness, and consequence reversals; before/after relabeling; less/more emphasis; paired gives/gets or evaluative claims; moralized blame-to-growth reframes; and evidence-then-limitation sequences. Judge the relationship expressed rather than requiring a listed construction.
- **Boundary:** Preserve informative corrections, distinctions, alternatives, role allocations, changed circumstances, scientific limitations, and narrative developments. Their useful difference can be concise, qualitative, or implicit in the complete passage. Ordinary negation, an additive "not only" construction, or two different actions alone does not establish a staged opposition.
- **Boundary:** Assess what the rejected side contributes beyond the preferred statement. Preserve useful exclusions, corrections, tradeoffs, and consequences; catch a foil that chiefly supplies rhetorical elevation even when the preferred statement contains details. Relevant context can make a terse opposition useful by establishing the misconception, decision, or consequence it resolves. Names, numbers, causal words, technical vocabulary, and added length protect only the distinction they explain.
- **Boundary:** Include direct or implied opponents, rhetorical questions, caricatured alternatives, anticipated accusations, and unnecessary denials before a substantive recommendation. Preserve relevant objections whose relevance follows from the supplied question, proposal, circumstances, or consequences; they need no named speaker or formal evidence. Judge the supplied writing rather than assuming an external audience's beliefs.

- **Legacy source:** `fragment-stacking`, `fragment-stack-detector.ts`, `elliptical-action-stack.ts` and its grammar guards.

### Formal Transition Density (`formal-transition-density`)

- **Input:** `passage`.
- **Catch:** At least three nearby sentence-prefacing connectors or emphasis markers repeatedly announce relations or significance while adding no useful organization to the local passage.
- Hit: "**Moreover**, we are committed to progress. **Furthermore**, our commitment remains strong. **Ultimately**, progress requires commitment." The markers stage a progression that the statements do not develop.
- No-hit: "The small trials found a benefit. However, the largest trial found none. Therefore, we report the trial sizes separately. Similarly, the appendix separates the two recruitment methods." Each marker communicates a distinct relevant relation.
- Preserve useful reasoning, comparison and chronology at the same frequency. Unrelated sections do not form a local stack merely because they share a formal register.
- **Legacy source:** `formal-transition-density` and its 23 openings. Generalize equivalent prefacing expressions; preserve necessary relations independently of their vocabulary.

### Triple-Word Repeat (`triple-word-repeat`)

- **Input:** `passage`.
- **Catch:** Three or more generic list members manufacture rhetorical completeness through broad praise, abstract virtues or parallel promises rather than communicate useful distinctions.
- Hit: "Our platform is **powerful, transformative, and inspiring**. It gives volunteers a calendar for choosing shifts." The enumeration supplies a generic promotional triad.
- No-hit: "The toolbox contains a hammer, a level, and a tape measure." A useful three-property description remains a no-hit when its distinct meanings are supplied in context.
- Require one coordinated enumeration with at least three members whose interchangeable roles manufacture completeness. Identify the list-level defect separately from the truth, strength or specificity of its claims; generic praise or missing explanation alone belongs to its existing check. A useful list of distinct properties remains a no-hit for this check even when one property deserves another judgment.
- Preserve the existing adjective, abstract-noun and rhetorical-verb branches while varying their words and number of members. Three items alone establish no defect.
- Judge formulaic enumeration separately from exaggerated claim strength or missing substantive content. Use the existing family when that is the sole defect; independently defective list packaging can coexist with other labels.
- **Legacy source:** `triple-word-repeat` and its vocabulary-driven list matcher. Its name describes the existing identifier; its actual pattern is a formulaic list rather than three occurrences of one word.

### Word Repetition (`word-repetition`)

- **Input:** `passage`.
- **Catch:** At least six uses of the same content word or inflected form in one prose paragraph create an avoidable lexical echo. Count the same sense and lexical use across sentence roles.
- Hit: "The **portal** gives volunteers a **portal** for bookings. The **portal** stores their preferences. Through the **portal** they update the **portal** profile and check the **portal** messages." The repeated component name dominates ordinary descriptions of its functions.
- No-hit: "Each sentence below is copied on its own into a separately signed maintenance entry and must identify the component within the instruction: The valve remains closed while its inlet is pressurized. Open the valve only after isolating the inlet. Record the serial number engraved on the valve before removal. Support the valve as its retaining bolts are loosened. Inspect the valve seat for scoring. Replace the valve if the seat is damaged." The supplied use explains why each independently used instruction retains its component name.
- Useful facts or distinct actions alone do not justify repeating an established name. Judge what the repeated naming contributes in the supplied writing; preserve it when it distinguishes competing referents or parts, identifies the component within instructions used independently, keeps comparison labels distinct, preserves a substantive terminology contrast, or supplies deliberate refrain.
- In ordinary procedures, preserve naming that maintains precision or organization; flag avoidable echo when a shared subject or clear reference serves the same function. Genre, procedural tone and checklist headings alone supply no exemption. Preserve examined wording at the same frequency and assess shorter content words beyond the legacy restriction.
- Keep paragraph boundaries: occurrences spread across independent paragraphs do not meet this pattern's count. Repetitions solely owned by a defective frame, predicate ending or connector belong to that more specific check; independently repeated wording elsewhere remains eligible.
- Count noun subjects, objects and noun modifiers together when they retain the same sense, including portal profile and portal messages. Combine ordinary verbal inflections of the same action, including verbal -ing forms; keep distinct senses, derived words and different noun, verb or adjective uses separate.
- Require six eligible occurrences after excluding recurrences whose entire defect belongs to an opening, ending or connector. Eligibility alone does not establish avoidable lexical echo.
- **Legacy source:** `word-repetition`. Retain the six-occurrence coverage while replacing membership-based exclusion with a context-sensitive judgment of useful recurrence.
