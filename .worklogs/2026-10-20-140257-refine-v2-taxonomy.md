# Refine V2 Taxonomy

## Summary

Refined the proposed taxonomy from seven broad families into twelve defect-specific families. Restored semantic thinness, separated puffery and unsupported authority, and defined shared types for empty takeaways and vague attribution.

## Decisions

- Classify "That is the real promise" and "The lesson was simple" as the same empty-takeaway issue. Generate data per consolidated type rather than per overlapping legacy detector.
- Separate inflated evaluation from pretended source support. Give staged contrasts, wordiness, and excessive hedging their own families.
- Preserve all 106 in-scope legacy identifiers for coverage accounting while separating their implementation boundaries from the proposed issue boundaries.
- Split behavior from broad old checks where required: generic signposting covers both empty claims and introductions; contrastive aphorism covers both standalone empty sayings and comparisons.
- Keep typography conditional, readability and terminology policy outside the iteration, and production behavior unchanged.

## Verification

- Read the overlapping signposting, lesson, affirmation, observer, emphasis, and contrast detectors and the relevant semantic pattern definitions.
- Checked Wikipedia's editorial distinction between puffery and unsupported attribution, and Purdue OWL's explanation of wordiness.
- The revised inventory contains twelve families, 106 mapped identifiers, and seven exclusions, with zero missing, extra, or duplicate identifiers.
- Prettier passes for the revised proposal.
- Independent review confirmed the family and behavior-split decisions. Replaced ambiguous boilerplate and hedging examples and clarified that inherited examples require V2 judgments before becoming labels.
