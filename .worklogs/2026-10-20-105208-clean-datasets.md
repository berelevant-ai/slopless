# Clean Datasets

## Summary

Replaced the mixed research tree with four prose collections in root `datasets/`: human, suspected AI, ordinary AI output, and synthetic examples. Each article or compact collection has Markdown text and a matching validated labels file.

## Decisions

- Preserved 15,608 existing articles and writing outputs byte-for-byte. Combined the 604 reserved paragraphs into one pair, preserving every original target, label, evidence quote, and coverage reference.
- Stored independent collection samples with identifiers and ranges, including 264,581 unlabeled upstream sentences. Sample boundaries remain available independently of editorial judgments.
- Retained original specificity scores, individual vagueness ratings, idiom judgments, and syntax annotations. Removed duplicated prose from annotation metadata while keeping the full text in Markdown.
- Kept 400 project-generated test articles under `ai-synthetic`, with unknown prompts recorded as unavailable. Preserved 18 ordinary generation/editing outputs under `ai-normal` with their documented provenance.
- Extracted generated adversarial passages, research examples, selected phrase judgments, and 421 historical semantic-thinness examples from the retired material.
- Preserved all 141 historical no-hit judgments: 82 attached to their articles and 59 retained as contextualized excerpts. Whole-article readability judgments cover the complete article; raw diagnostic excerpts remain in their annotations.
- Replaced eight review transcripts and three reports with 45 relevant review notes beside their labels. Original author judgments remain unchanged, including disputed records.
- Removed the five old dataset roots, their inventory, downloaded lexicons/packages, image ratings, old reports, audit predictions, request/response dumps, and compressed experiment history from the working tree. Commit `41d34169` retains the historical material.
- Retired 39 experiment-specific executable helpers and two obsolete downloaded-source/layout verifiers. The fixture/corpus verifier now uses paired-data validation while retaining every application coverage check.
- Ported the fresh-corpus size and catchability verifiers to paired datasets and retained their original thresholds. Their term-policy configuration now lives with behavior inputs rather than writing datasets.
- Added pinned development dependency `ajv@8.20.0`, already present transitively, for JSON Schema validation. Updated CI to validate datasets and execute their contract tests on Node 22 and 24.
- Kept the existing forbidden-terms source, exports, fixture configuration, and approved-output edits outside this commit. Production rule behavior and the four prompt texts are unchanged.
