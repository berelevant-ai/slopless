# Why do people save so much?

When people over 60 reflect back on their life, they generally do  not  say:
I wish I took out more student loans.
I wish I bought more expensive cars.
I wish I bought fancier clothes.
I wish I didn't save so much money for retirement.
In fact, it's almost always the opposite. Why? It's because your question is a good one and that same compelling argument drives most people to act like you suggest. The key though is in this statement of yours:
Money is what I need/want  now .
It turns out that this statement is true every single day of your life, including during retirement.
