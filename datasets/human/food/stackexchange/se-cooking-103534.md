# Why is there potato in meatballs?

With absolutely no citation or evidence to back up my claim ;-) I'm going to say that the reasoning is two- if not three-fold…. or maybe more...
It's cheaper. It adds bulk.
It helps the meatball stay whole; a binder. Similar reasoning as breadcrumbs &/or egg, but see 3.
The binder is needed because the EU allows the meat producer to add 10% water to the meat before it's even completed the initial butchery stage [1] . Therefore you need some 'soak-up' to prevent the water being squeezed out as soon as you start to heat the product.
It helps the texture; stops it becoming too chewy. A mouth-feel thing, same as you might make 50% beef/50% pork meatballs. 100% beef can be a bit heavy.
