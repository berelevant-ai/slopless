# Why can't Google understand why I don't want to combine my Gmail and Youtube?

<!-- author: hackaflocka; created: 2013-12-27T15:52:48Z; score: 19 -->

2 decades ago, a few people told me about how difficult it was to get a job at Microsoft, that they only hired the smartest, most intelligent people. Well, we've seen the result of that. Completely garbage products.<p>I'm afraid, the same movie might be played out with Google now. Once more this morning, Google stealthily tried to get me to create a Google+ page, and to get me to combine my YouTube username and my Gmail username.<p>I use that Gmail address to email people I don't know (e.g., for Craigslist related stuff). If they knew what kinds of videos I watch, it might give them a negotiating advantage, because they know certain traits of my personality (nothing scandalous, I promise ya!).<p>Related: have we made progress on that open source self-hosted alternative to Gmail yet?
