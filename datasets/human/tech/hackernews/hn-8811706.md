# Ask HN: Done with startups. Contemplating freelancing

<!-- author: consowels; created: 2014-12-29T20:53:26Z; score: 21 -->

(I'm a semi-regular HN participant using a burner login to hide my identity.) I've spent all (15+ years) of my career at startups. Several times I've been a single-digit employee and have unsuccessfully founded a company. I've had one modest pay day but that money is long gone. I think I've had enough. I've grown tired of the ups/downs and long hours (50 to 60 hours a week.) My imagination is no longer captured by prospects of changing the world or a big pay day. I'm exploring the world of freelancing. I'm thinking I could work 4 days a week on average on paying projects (Ruby, Rails, iOS, C) and spend one day a week working on open source or other side projects (which may or may not generate revenue.) In general, looking for an environment where I have more autonomy and better work/life balance. Has anyone successfully made this kind of transition? Many thanks.
