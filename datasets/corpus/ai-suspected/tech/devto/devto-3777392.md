# I got tired of useless news, so I built a Firefox extension for Dev.to

Imagine opening your browser and being flooded with generic ads, clickbait news, or drama you don't care about. As developers, we want to stay updated with high-quality tech content without the noise.

That’s exactly why I decided to build my very first browser extension: Dev.to News Feed.

🚀 **What it does**

It brings the latest, trendiest, and most important tech blogs from the Dev.to community directly into your Firefox browser. No distractions, just pure developer knowledge.


![Dev.to News Feed in action](https://dev-to-uploads.s3.amazonaws.com/uploads/articles/zf3ygv98c3gl2oi5yu2d.png)


🧠 **The Journey**

This was my first time coding a browser extension, and honestly, it was an amazing learning experience. But the absolute best part? Real users are already installing it and adding it to their daily workflows. Knowing that something I built is helping other engineers is an incredible feeling.

If you are a Firefox user and want a clean tech feed, you can add it here:
👉 [Dev.to News Feed on Firefox Add-ons](https://addons.mozilla.org/it/firefox/addon/dev-to-news-feed/)

I’d love to hear your thoughts, feedback, or feature requests. What should I add next?
