## Project Background

### Overview

FreeDomain is a flagship project of the DigitalPlat Foundation, founded and maintained by Edward Hsing. What started as a small DNS experiment at age 15 (providing subdomains for friends) has evolved into a mature platform serving hundreds of thousands of users globally.

To date, FreeDomain has facilitated over **500,000** domain registrations and has amassed a staggering **172k+** Stars on GitHub, making it one of the most popular digital inclusion projects on the platform.

### Core Value Props

1. **Zero Entry Cost**: No credit card, no monthly fees—truly free domain registration.
2. **Full Sovereignty**: Despite being free, these domains allow you to delegate management to your own DNS provider (Cloudflare, FreeDNS, etc.), granting you full control over your records.
3. **Safety & Transparency**: As an open-source project, it places a heavy emphasis on abuse reporting and community-driven security notices.

## Key Features

### Available Extensions

FreeDomain currently offers several extensions to choose from:

- **.DPDNS.ORG**: The official recommended extension.
- **.US.KG**: A highly recognizable second-level domain.
- **.QZZ.IO** / **.XX.KG** / **.QD.JE**: Versatile options for various creative projects.

### Core Capabilities

- **Custom Delegation**: Supports binding to top-tier DNS management platforms.
- **Community Trust**: Over 500,000 active registrations testify to its reliability.
- **Simplified Dashboard**: A dedicated, easy-to-use management interface for quick registration.
- **Open-Source Governance**: Documentation and core logic are open, encouraging transparency.

### Quick Start

1. **Access the Dashboard**: Visit the [DigitalPlat FreeDomain Dashboard](https://dash.domain.digitalplat.org/).
2. **Search and Claim**: Search for your desired prefix; if available, follow the prompts to register.
3. **Configure DNS**: Enter your nameservers (e.g., from Cloudflare) in the dashboard management view.
4. **Validation**: Once DNS propagates, your new domain is ready for use, including HTTPS support.

## Technical Deep Dive

### From a 15-Year-Old’s Experiment to 500k Users

The success of FreeDomain is built on addressing "digital barriers" while managing significant operational complexity.

1. **Abuse Mitigation**: Free domains are often targeted for phishing. DigitalPlat has implemented a robust email-reporting and automated filtering system to keep the ecosystem clean.
2. **Trust & Relationships**: By partnering with low-cost or non-profit Root Domain (TID) registries and funneling donations back into infrastructure, they've created a sustainable loop.
3. **Radical Simplicity**: No complex KYC or hidden fees. This dramatically lowers the barrier for students and developers in underserved regions.
