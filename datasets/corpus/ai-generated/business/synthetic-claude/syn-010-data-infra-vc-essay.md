# The Modern Data Stack Is Dead. Long Live the Intelligent Data Platform.

It feels like just yesterday that the modern data stack was the hottest narrative in enterprise infrastructure. Ingest with Fivetran. Transform with dbt. Store in Snowflake. Visualize with Looker. The composable, best-of-breed approach to analytics infrastructure felt like the obvious answer to the limitations of the old monolithic BI suite.

And then AI happened. And everything changed.

Notably, the composable stack was optimized for a world where humans were the primary consumers of data. Dashboards for the analyst. Reports for the executive. But when the consumer of data is an AI agent - operating in milliseconds, at massive scale, across unstructured and structured sources simultaneously - the composable stack's seams start to show. Importantly, latency compounds, data quality issues cascade, and the cost of orchestrating twelve different tools through a single query path becomes prohibitive.

What paves the way forward is a new architecture: the Intelligent Data Platform. Not a suite from a legacy vendor with AI bolted on. A new category, built from the ground up for a world where AI is both a consumer and a producer of data at every layer of the pipeline.

The companies we are most excited about in this space are doing something that looks almost counterintuitive: they are bringing the layers back together, but selectively and intelligently. Real-time ingestion, semantic layer, governance, and inference in a single coherent system. The secret sauce is not the reunification per se - it is the governance layer that makes federation trustworthy and the semantic layer that makes data AI-consumable without hand-holding.

It is worth noting that this market is early and the winners are not obvious yet. That is precisely why we are leaning in aggressively. The infrastructure layer always gets rebuilt when the compute paradigm shifts. This is that moment.
