# Series B Announcement: What We Are Building and Why This Moment Matters

To the Lattice community -

Today we are announcing our $47 million Series B, led by Emergence Capital with participation from our existing investors at Benchmark and Sequoia. I want to explain what we are going to do with it, because we have been deliberately quiet about our roadmap and I think this moment calls for transparency.

The short version: we are building the governance layer that the modern data stack has always been missing, and we believe the market has just reached the moment where this problem has gone from "important and deferrable" to "urgent and non-negotiable." Notably, the catalyst is AI. Specifically, the explosion of AI workloads that consume and produce data at a scale and pace that makes the data quality, lineage, and access control problems that were manageable in a human-speed data environment suddenly existential.

Here is what I mean. When a human analyst queries your data warehouse, they bring judgment. They notice when a number looks wrong. They know which columns to trust and which ones to treat with skepticism. They have context. When an AI agent queries your data warehouse - and increasingly, AI agents are querying your data warehouse hundreds of times a day - it has none of that context. It will act confidently on bad data. And the decisions it makes based on that bad data will compound before any human notices something is wrong.

This is the problem Lattice solves. Importantly, not by adding another governance dashboard to your existing stack. By building governance as a runtime layer that sits in the query path, validates data quality at access time, enforces access policies dynamically, and maintains auditable lineage for every data asset that any AI or human workload touches.

It is worth noting that this is a technically hard problem that requires deep integration with existing data infrastructure. We have spent two years building those integrations, and they are the foundation of a durable competitive position. The force multiplier of a runtime governance layer is that it gets more valuable with every new data source and every new AI workload - and both of those are growing.

We are excited about what comes next. Thank you for being part of this.

- Kavya
Co-founder & CEO, Lattice
