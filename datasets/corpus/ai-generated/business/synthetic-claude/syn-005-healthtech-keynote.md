# Keynote Abstract: Reimagining Care Delivery in the Age of Intelligent Health Platforms

The healthcare system as we know it is at an inflection point. Fragmented data, siloed workflows, and administrative overhead are not just operational headaches - they are patient safety issues. In this keynote, I will argue that the convergence of ambient AI, interoperable health records, and real-time clinical decision support is not merely an incremental step forward. It ushers in a new era of care delivery that was simply not possible five years ago.

Notably, the organizations that will thrive in this new landscape are not the ones with the biggest IT budgets. They are the ones with the clearest vision for how to empower clinicians, reduce cognitive load, and drive value across the care continuum. Importantly, the evidence is already emerging from early adopters who have deployed AI-native clinical workflows at scale: outcomes improve, burnout decreases, and the economics of care delivery shift in ways that benefit both patients and providers.

This session will explore three paradigm-shifting ideas. First, ambient intelligence as a force multiplier for the clinician at the point of care. Second, the role of best-of-breed interoperability standards in making health data finally actionable at scale. Third, and perhaps most crucially, the ethical architecture required to deploy these systems in ways that earn and maintain patient trust.

It is worth noting that the technology is no longer the limiting factor. The limiting factor is organizational will and a clear-eyed understanding of where AI enhances human judgment and where it must remain squarely in a supporting role. The secret sauce is not the algorithm - it is the change management that surrounds it.

Join me for a candid look at what the best health systems are doing right, what the rest of us can learn, and what a genuinely patient-centered AI future looks like in practice.
