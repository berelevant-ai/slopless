# EdTech Dispatch: The Personalization Promise Is Finally Being Kept

Good morning, educators and edtech watchers.

For as long as personalized learning has been a buzzword in our industry, the gap between the promise and the delivery has been the source of endless frustration. Adaptive systems that adapted to three variables. Personalization that meant "choose your own reading level." Recommendations that were marginally better than random. We have all sat through the demos. We have all read the white papers. And most of us have watched the pilots produce results that were encouraging in controlled settings and disappointing at scale.

Something has changed in the last eighteen months, and this week we are trying to make sense of exactly what.

Notably, the convergence of genuinely capable AI tutoring, high-quality content generation at scale, and learning analytics that now have enough granularity to be predictive rather than descriptive has created conditions that did not exist before. Importantly, we are seeing early outcome data from a small number of deployments that is unlike anything the industry has produced in the adaptive learning category to date. The effect sizes are not "statistically significant improvement over control." They are the kind of numbers that make experienced researchers do a double-take and call for replication.

The critical question - and we dig into this at length inside - is whether these results will hold as deployments move from controlled pilots to district-wide implementations. The history of edtech is littered with pilot successes that collapsed under the weight of real-world complexity. It is worth noting that the companies producing the strongest results right now are the ones that have built their products with that complexity as the starting assumption, not an afterthought.

Inside this issue: a deep dive on the three platforms generating the most compelling outcome data right now, an interview with a district curriculum director who has run more adaptive learning pilots than anyone we know, and our take on what the research actually says about AI tutoring efficacy.

Buckle up.
