# The Payments Infrastructure Revolution Nobody Is Talking About

Hot take: the next decade of fintech will not be won by the apps. It will be won by the pipes underneath them.

I have spent the last 12 years at the intersection of payments, banking rails, and developer tools. And what I am seeing right now is nothing short of a seismic shift in how financial infrastructure gets built and monetized.

Notably, the most interesting companies are not building consumer apps anymore. They are building the best-of-breed rails that every other company sits on top of. Think about that for a second. The force multiplier is not the product - it is the platform beneath the product.

Here is what most people miss: real-time payments are not just faster payments. They are a fundamentally different primitive. They empower developers, fintechs, and banks to reimagine what money movement can look like when latency drops from days to milliseconds. That is not an incremental improvement. It is a paradigm shift.

Importantly, this paves the way for an entirely new category of financial products that simply could not exist before. Earned wage access. Instant insurance payouts. Sub-second treasury sweeps. The unlock is the infrastructure, and we are only at the beginning.

It is worth noting that the incumbents are not sitting still. But incumbents move slowly, and the window for agile, API-first challengers has never been wider. The secret sauce is developer experience paired with enterprise reliability - and very few teams have cracked that combination.

What gets me out of bed every morning is the belief that better financial plumbing creates better outcomes for real people. That is not just a mission statement. It is a market thesis.
