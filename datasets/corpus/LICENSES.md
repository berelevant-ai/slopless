# Corpus licenses and publishability (master index)

A measurement corpus for the slopless linter, organized by provenance:

```
article/corpus/
  human/<topic>/<source>/        human / pre-AI (false-positive oracle)
  ai-generated/<topic>/<model>/  generated by us (true-positive set)
  ai-suspected/<topic>/<source>/ recent scraped, likely AI (true-positive candidates)
```

**Gitignored** in the public slopless repo; intended for a **private repo**. Each source
directory has its own `LICENSE.md`. Only rows marked **Publishable = Yes** may be released
(under the stated license/attribution); copyrighted sources stay internal.

Totals: human ~21.6M words (13,680 docs), ai-generated ~187k words (400 docs),
ai-suspected ~1.6M words (1,500 docs).

## Obligations for the publishable subset

- **CC BY-SA** (Wikipedia, Stack Exchange, MDN): attribute source + contributors; license
  derivatives CC BY-SA.
- **CC BY 2.5** (Wikinews): attribute Wikinews.
- **Public domain** (Gutenberg, Federal Register, MedlinePlus, PEPs, CourtListener opinions):
  no obligation.
- **IETF RFCs**: verbatim only.
- **arXiv/PubMed**: per-item; treat as internal unless an item is clearly CC.
- **Copyrighted** (Paul Graham, a16z, dev.to): internal measurement only, not redistributed.
- **AI-generated**: ours; label as synthetic and note single-model (Claude).
