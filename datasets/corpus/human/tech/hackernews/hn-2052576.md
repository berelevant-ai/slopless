# Rate my Startup: Quicklyst, a note-taking application

<!-- author: shantanubala; created: 2010-12-30T16:20:57Z; score: 5 -->

Hello HN. I'd like to thank you all for the sheer amount of resources and discussions available on this site that are just a search away. They're great for helping make decisions. I just launched Quicklyst in the spirit of "launch a product you're embarrassed about" and "launch early." Tell me what you think. http://www.quicklyst.com/ It's a note-taking app that has an outline-style textbox. It has automatic Kindle delivery for notes (yeah, I copied Instapaper on that one), printable study guides, and a DuckDuckGo zero-click info feature. I tried my best to keep the interface simple, but I have explanations of the features at: http://www.quicklyst.com/guide And one question: Should I go freemium? I figure it's easy to switch to freemium, but hard to switch back.
