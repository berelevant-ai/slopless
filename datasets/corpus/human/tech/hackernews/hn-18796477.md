# Ask HN: How did 2018 go for you? What went well? What didn't?

<!-- author: acconrad; created: 2018-12-31T19:41:48Z; score: 22 -->

For me, what went well: * I started a consulting business and made nearly $300k in revenue * I shipped my first real SaaS project * I read over 40 books * I started a growing blog and a newsletter * I landed on my first major podcast (>25k subscribers) What didn't go well (what I want to improve in 2019): * Consulting isn't for me (at a full-time capacity). A product-based business scales better than a service-based business and if I continued down my consulting path I would work myself to the bone. I'm back working for an employer full time until my SaaS projects can pay the bills. * Lots of injuries (multiple tendinopathies and impingements from lifting). Working with a PT to lift smarter and diagnose problems sooner. * Didn't ship my 20+ part algorithms tutorial series/book. Plan to have that completed (first draft) by end of January 2019. * Reduce the number of concurrent commitments. Especially towards Q4 2018 I was overwhelmed with too much concurrency in my life and I need to learn to focus on fewer, higher-value things. * Failed to really take hold of daily gratitude and meditation practices. I can no longer avoid the fact that I need to integrate this in my life and so what better time than now to start daily gratitude and meditation to improve my mental health, clarity, and performance. Happy New Year Hacker News!
