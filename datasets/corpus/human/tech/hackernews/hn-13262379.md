# Ask HN: Why is still so hard to detect spam?

<!-- author: nicc; created: 2016-12-27T10:29:27Z; score: 5 -->

I'm using macOS Mail as a secondary client alongside Google Inbox (which works great), and it's amazing how bad the spam filter is. I've been training it to detect spam by marking emails manually for about 2 weeks, and it's still having a hard time recognising most spam emails. I know that spammers get clever trying to trick spam filters, but I feel like some emails are really a no-brainer they're spam: for instance, I'm getting hundreds of email in Chinese, and I don't even have a Chinese font installed (which is information I assume macOS could potentially have access to). What are the chances it's not spam?
