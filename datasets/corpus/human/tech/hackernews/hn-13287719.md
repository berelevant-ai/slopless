# Ask HN: How to survive a year at my job while burned out

<!-- author: rizizep; created: 2016-12-30T21:43:19Z; score: 6 -->

I've been really burned out for more than a year and feel really crappy going to work every day. I am not burned out by software engineering, in fact I enjoy it a lot. It's just the way the company is managed: poor processes, bad hires, plenty of good people leaving. At the same time, there is a pretty big financial incentive in place if I somehow survive here for another year. It's a pretty significant amount of money, so I find it very hard to justify leaving now, even though I really want to. But one year is a pretty long time to be unhappy. Has anyone been in a similar situation lately? Any tips on how to make this extra year tolerable?
