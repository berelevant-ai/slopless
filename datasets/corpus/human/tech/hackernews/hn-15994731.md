# Ask HN: How to you deal with a science denier?

<!-- author: hellofunk; created: 2017-12-23T16:15:00Z; score: 6 -->

I'm having great difficulty finding a way to internally calm the bizarre and entirely unique form of stress and frustration and confusion that comes from interacting with people who emphatically refuse to believe in basic scientific facts or theories that are considered fundamental to the modern world, primarily climate change and evolution. Over and over again I hear from people, especially over the holidays when I interact with those I normally do not, that a cold snow storm is "proof" that there is no global warming. Some of these same people have similar denials about any genetic similarities between a human and a chimp, for example. No matter what angle I attempt in trying to "fix" their perception, nothing works. It seems both impossible and genuinely incredible that some people (many people) are so convinced they are correct in things they actually have no personal understanding of. How do you deal with such an environment?
