# Tell HN: This season is not merry for everyone

<!-- author: hluska; created: 2015-12-25T00:30:39Z; score: 7 -->

Sometimes, amidst all the 'happy holidays' greetings, there is a tendency to forget that this season is not necessarily happy for everyone. To non-Christian people, December 25 is a day like any other. But, for people who are lonely, whether they are estranged from their families or whatever, this season can be intensely sad and difficult. It can be even tougher because of the immense marketing that accompanies this season. 7 Cups of Tea is an amazing service. I haven't looked into this, but I assume that they have listeners available to chat. They're good people - reach out. For what it's worth, if this time of year is hard for you, I am very sorry. I wish you the absolute best. Be safe and peaceful.
