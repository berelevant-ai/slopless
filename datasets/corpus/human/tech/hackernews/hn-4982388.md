# Ask HN: Feedback about HN itself. What do you think?

<!-- author: JoelMarsh; created: 2012-12-29T15:55:54Z; score: 5 -->

I like the content of this site, but I also have a few suggestions, based on a few months as a regular visitor: 1) Make internal links smarter. If I read a page for 10 minutes and then click "next" I often get an error. Not an error page. Just the error text. I actually got this error when trying to submit this post. 2) Please implement a "collapse" button on all 1st-level comments. In big conversations I want to skip to the next comment when I am not interested. I don't want to scroll slowly down the page to find it. 3) Please add a "Show" tab to the main navigation, similar to "Ask". If anybody needs a little extra exposure, it's the people who are releasing new stuff. 4) Make the voting buttons bigger, and allow "undo". I think you'll agree that "tiny disappearing buttons" doesn't have that "awesome UX" ring to it. Especially when they are the primary user action that fuels the function of the site. 5) I see that "saved stories" is a possibility in my profile, but I have yet to find the button/link that allows me to save stories. Does it exist? 6) You're gonna hate this one, but I really wish there was a way to see when someone has replied to my comments. i.e. — Notifications. I would gladly sacrifice the "threads" and "comments" pages for a tab that has something similar but only relevant to me. 7) Does karma have any effect on anything, or is it just gamification? I can't see the scores of comments or people in context, therefore they are useless to me. My own karma points have more use as a notification (if it goes up, something happened) than anything else. If karma creates influence... a little info somewhere would be awesome (in my "dashboard" maybe?). Thank you.
