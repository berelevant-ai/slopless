It is shown as {Rx} in this version of the etext. Some letters have a macron (straight line) above them; these are indicated as {=x}, with x being the particular letter. The book uses both upper and lower case oe ligatures. These are shown as {OE} and {oe} respectively. The many inconsistencies in hyphenation and use of accents and ligatures have been preserved as printed, with a few exceptions. Variable and archaic spelling has also been preserved. A full list of amendments and other notes follow the end of the book. A considerable number of the recipe and page numbers in the index are incorrect; however, they have been preserved as printed.
