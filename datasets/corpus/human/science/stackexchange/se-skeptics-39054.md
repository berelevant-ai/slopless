# Did JFK call himself a jelly donut in his famous Berlin speech?

No, it wasn't interpreted that way.  Berliner is a name for a doughnut, similar to a Frankfurter being a type of sausage.  However, it does also mean living in/being from Berlin, similar to Londoner.
See this article,  The Real Meaning of Ich Bin ein Berliner , as told by the director of the John F. Kennedy Presidential Library and Museum:
Afterward it would be suggested that Kennedy had got the translation
wrong—that by using the article ein before the word Berliner, he had
mistakenly called himself a jelly doughnut. In fact, Kennedy was
correct. To state Ich bin Berliner would have suggested being born in
Berlin, whereas adding the word ein implied being a Berliner in
spirit. His audience understood that he meant to show his solidarity.
