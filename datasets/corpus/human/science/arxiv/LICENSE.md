## License

Per-paper. arXiv itself does not hold copyright on submissions. Each paper is licensed by its author(s) under one of:
- arXiv non-exclusive distribution license (most common; does not grant redistribution rights to third parties)
- Creative Commons (CC BY, CC BY-SA, CC BY-NC, CC0, etc.) -- marked in individual paper metadata on arxiv.org
- Other author-retained licenses

To determine the exact license for any individual paper, check the license field on its abstract page (https://arxiv.org/abs/<id>).
