Appellant appeals from the district court's order denying relief on his 42 U.S.C. &#167; 1983 (1988) complaint. We have reviewed the record and the district court's opinion and find no reversible error. Accordingly, we affirm on the reasoning of the district court. Moore v. Byrd, No. CA-95-2010-WMN (D. Md. Feb. 29, 1996; Apr. 4, 1996).
