**Register:** Human legal/judicial prose - US court opinions

**License:** US public domain  
US court opinions are government edicts and are not subject to copyright under 17 USC. The Supreme Court confirmed in Georgia v. Public.Resource.Org, 590 U.S. 255 (2020) that works created by government officials in the course of their official duties cannot be copyrighted.

**Redistribute:** Yes  
**Commercial use:** Yes  
**Publishable:** Yes (public domain)

**Date range:** Predominantly pre-2021. Source file covers 1975-2023; opinions with IDs above 6M that contained 2022 or later filing dates were excluded. No date metadata was available per-opinion in the bulk export; filtering applied by ID range and year mentions in opinion text.

**Attribution courtesy:** CourtListener / Free Law Project requests attribution when using their data. Opinions were obtained from CourtListener's public bulk data export. CourtListener is operated by the Free Law Project, a 501(c)(3) non-profit. See https://free.law/ for more information.

**API note:** The CourtListener REST API (https://www.courtlistener.com/api/rest/v4/) requires a free account token. This corpus was built from the public S3 bulk data export, which is identical in content to the API data and is provided without authentication requirements.
