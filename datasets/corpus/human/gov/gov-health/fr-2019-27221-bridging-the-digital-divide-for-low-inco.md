Synopsis I. Introduction 1. For years, the Commission has been taking steps to address waste, fraud, and abuse in the program, including through the establishment of a National Lifeline Eligibility Verifier. The Commission continues that work to strengthen the Lifeline program. Specifically, seeking comments on appropriate program goals and metrics for a modernized Lifeline [[Page 71339]] program and additional improvements to program integrity. II. Discussion 2. The Commission seeks comments on continuing to improve the operation and oversight of the Lifeline program; seeks comments on adding the goal of increasing broadband adoption for consumers who would not otherwise subscribe to broadband as one of the Lifeline program's goals and also seeks comments on making additional program integrity improvements to the program and establishing privacy training requirements for entities accessing personal information in the NLAD. 3.
