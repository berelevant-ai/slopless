# In the Matter of Team Industrial Services, Inc.; Establishment of Atomic Safety and Licensing Board

(Order Imposing Civil Monetary Penalty) This Board is being established pursuant to a hearing request submitted by Team Industrial Services, Inc. in response to an Order Imposing Civil Monetary Penalty, EA-18-124, dated September 20, 2019 and published in the Federal Register. See 84 FR 58178 (Oct 30, 2019). The Board is comprised of the following Administrative Judges: Paul S.
