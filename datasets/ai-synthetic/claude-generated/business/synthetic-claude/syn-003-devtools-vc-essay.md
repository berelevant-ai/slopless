# Why Developer Tools Are Eating the Enterprise (And Why Now)

The developer is the new kingmaker. That is not a metaphor - it is a market reality reshaping how enterprise software gets bought, deployed, and scaled. At our firm, we have been tracking the developer tools space for over a decade, and the opportunity set in front of us today is genuinely unprecedented.

Notably, the shift started with the cloud. Infrastructure became programmable. Suddenly the best-of-breed way to build was bottoms-up - start with one developer who loves your product, and let organic adoption do the rest. Importantly, this inverted the traditional sales motion and in doing so created an entirely new go-to-market playbook that the savviest founders are now running to remarkable effect.

What excites us at this particular moment in the cycle is the emergence of AI-native developer tooling. This is not AI bolted onto existing workflows as an afterthought. These are tools built from first principles for a world where code generation, review, and deployment are augmented at every step. The force multiplier for an AI-assisted engineering team of 10 is, by our estimates, approaching what a traditional team of 40 could accomplish. That compression is extraordinary and it paves the way for a fundamentally different kind of engineering organization.

It is worth noting that the companies winning in this space share a common set of traits: they obsess over developer experience, they move fast and break things in their own products so their customers do not have to, and they treat documentation as a first-class product surface.

The market window is open. Infrastructure primitives are being repriced. And the founders who understand both the technical depth and the distribution dynamics stand to build generational companies. We are actively looking for the builders shaping this new era.
