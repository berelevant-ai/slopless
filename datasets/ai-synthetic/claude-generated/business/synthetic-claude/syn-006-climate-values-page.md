# Who We Are

At Verdant, we believe that the most important problem of our generation demands the most ambitious thinking of our generation. Climate change is not a future threat - it is a present emergency, and we are here to meet it with technology, urgency, and an unrelenting commitment to impact.

We are a team of scientists, engineers, operators, and optimists who came together because we saw a gap between the pace of the problem and the pace of the solutions. Importantly, we did not build Verdant to be another climate company. We built it to be the climate company - the one that ushers in a new era of measurable, verifiable, permanent carbon reduction at a scale that actually moves the needle on global emissions.

Our values are not words on a wall. They are the operating system of how we make decisions every single day.

**Move with urgency.** The climate clock does not pause for process. We move fast and we move smart, because the cost of inaction is not abstract - it is measured in lives and ecosystems.

**Empower every stakeholder.** The transition to a decarbonized economy has to work for everyone - workers, communities, investors, and the planet. We design every solution with that full picture in mind.

**Make the invisible visible.** Carbon is notoriously hard to measure. Our secret sauce is the combination of satellite telemetry, IoT sensor networks, and machine learning that lets us quantify what was previously a guess. That precision is a force multiplier for every climate action we take or enable.

**Earn trust through transparency.** We publish our methodology. We show our work. In a space full of greenwashing, radical honesty is a competitive advantage.

Notably, the hardest problems attract the best people. And we are just getting started.
