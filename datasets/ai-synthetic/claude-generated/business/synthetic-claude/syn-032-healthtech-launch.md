# Introducing CareFlow 4.0: The Operating System for Value-Based Care

The shift from fee-for-service to value-based care is the most significant structural transformation in American healthcare in a generation. It is also one of the most operationally complex transitions any health system leadership team will ever undertake. Today, we are releasing CareFlow 4.0 - the platform purpose-built to make that transition not just manageable, but a genuine competitive advantage.

CareFlow 4.0 is not an EHR module. It is not a data warehouse with a care management dashboard bolted on. It is the operating system that sits between your clinical data and your value-based contract performance, translating one into the other in real time.

Notably, the core innovation in CareFlow 4.0 is our Contract Intelligence Layer, which ingests your value-based contracts - all of them, across all payers - and creates a continuous, real-time picture of performance against every quality metric, utilization target, and total cost of care benchmark that affects your financial outcomes. Importantly, that picture does not sit in a report that someone reads once a quarter. It surfaces at the point of care, in the workflow, when clinicians can still act on it.

Our secret sauce is the combination of contract ingestion that handles the wild variability of real-world VBC contract structures and a workflow integration layer that connects contract performance to clinical decision support without requiring a separate application to open. This is what empowers primary care teams to manage populations proactively rather than reactively - and it is what drives the outcome improvements that make value-based contracts financially viable.

It is worth noting that CareFlow 4.0 supports every major EHR platform through FHIR R4 APIs, and deploys in a typical health system environment in under 8 weeks. The era of value-based care technology that is too complex to actually use is over. Request a demo today.
