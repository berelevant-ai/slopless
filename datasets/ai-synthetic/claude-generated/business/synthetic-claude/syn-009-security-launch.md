# Announcing ShieldOps 3.0: Proactive Security for the AI-Powered Enterprise

The threat landscape has changed. Attackers are faster, more automated, and more sophisticated than at any point in history. Traditional reactive security models - detect, respond, remediate - are simply not built for the speed and scale of modern attacks. Today, we are proud to announce ShieldOps 3.0, our most significant platform release ever, and the one that finally puts defenders back ahead of the threat.

Notably, ShieldOps 3.0 is not a feature update. It is a ground-up rethinking of what enterprise security operations can look like when AI is not an add-on but the foundation. Importantly, we have rebuilt our detection engine from first principles, replacing static rule sets with a behavioral intelligence layer that learns continuously from your environment. The result is threat detection that gets smarter every day, not stale.

What is the secret sauce? Honestly, it is the data. ShieldOps processes over 2 trillion signals per day across our customer network. That scale of telemetry - anonymized, federated, and privacy-preserving - is a force multiplier for every threat model we run. When a new attack pattern emerges anywhere in our network, every customer benefits within minutes.

It is worth noting that we have also completely overhauled the analyst experience. Alert fatigue is one of the most serious challenges in the SOC today. ShieldOps 3.0 introduces Prioritized Investigation Queues, which use our AI reasoning layer to surface only the alerts that require human attention, with full context pre-assembled. This empowers analysts to do the deep investigative work they trained for, rather than drowning in noise.

ShieldOps 3.0 paves the way for a security operation that is not just faster but genuinely proactive. The era of chasing attackers is over. It is time to get ahead of them.
