# Threat Intel Weekly: When AI Attacks AI

Good morning, security practitioners.

We are entering a phase of the AI era that the security community has been warning about for two years, and now has to actually deal with: AI-powered offensive tools are no longer a research curiosity. They are in active use by threat actors ranging from nation-state groups to opportunistic criminal organizations, and the pace of capability development on the offensive side is genuinely alarming.

This week's issue is dedicated to unpacking what that means practically - not in the abstract, but in the specific, concrete ways that AI-assisted attacks are changing the operational reality for defenders right now.

Notably, the three most significant near-term threat vectors are AI-generated phishing that is sophisticated enough to defeat the behavioral heuristics that enterprise email security has relied on for years, AI-assisted vulnerability discovery that dramatically compresses the window between CVE disclosure and active exploitation, and AI-powered social engineering at scale that can target hundreds of employees simultaneously with personalized manipulation tailored to publicly available information about each target. Importantly, none of these are hypothetical - all three are documented in incidents from the past six months.

The force multiplier on the defensive side is AI-powered behavioral detection that operates at the scale and speed required to match the offensive threat. The challenge is that deploying this effectively requires a level of environmental telemetry that many organizations have not yet achieved, and the configuration and tuning required to reduce false positive rates to manageable levels is non-trivial.

It is worth noting that the security vendors making the strongest claims about AI-powered defense deserve the same rigorous evaluation framework you would apply to any security control. The gap between marketing and efficacy in this space is significant.

Inside: a detailed technical breakdown of three recent AI-assisted attacks, a practical guide to evaluating AI detection platforms, and an interview with a red team lead on what they are seeing in the wild.

Stay sharp out there.
