# From Our CEO: A Milestone, a Thank You, and What Comes Next

To our customers, our team, and the community that has grown up around Gridline -

Last week we crossed $10 million in annual recurring revenue. I want to pause and say: this number matters to me not because of what it represents on a cap table, but because of what it represents in terms of real organizations - real teams - doing more with the software we built together.

Notably, we did not get here by moving fast and breaking things. We got here by moving deliberately and fixing things. Some of those things were in the product. A lot of them were in ourselves - our assumptions about who our customer was, what they actually needed, and where we were unconsciously building for our own preferences rather than theirs.

The biggest lesson of this chapter: our customers are not looking for a force multiplier in the abstract. They are looking for specific, concrete outcomes in workflows they run every day. Importantly, when we stopped talking about what Gridline "enables" and started talking about what it specifically eliminates - the meeting, the spreadsheet, the manual handoff - our conversion rate improved materially. Precision beats promise every time.

Looking ahead, the next chapter is about depth, not breadth. We have a clear picture of the additional workflows where Gridline can drive value, and we are going to build them thoughtfully rather than rushing to expand the surface area. It is worth noting that we are deliberately not adding headcount ahead of need. We believe a small, focused team with clear priorities builds better software than a large team with diffuse ones.

Thank you for being part of this. The best of what we are building together is still ahead.

- Priya
Founder & CEO, Gridline
