# A Note From Our CEO: What We Are Building and Why It Matters

Team, investors, and friends of Learnpath -

Six months ago I stood in front of our seed investors and made a bet: that the traditional learning management system was not broken - it was obsolete. That the right answer was not to fix the LMS. It was to build something categorically different. Something that treats every learner as an individual, not a row in a database.

Today I want to share where we are and, more importantly, where we are going.

Notably, our traction numbers have exceeded every internal projection we set at the start of the year. We crossed 50,000 active learners last week, our 7-day retention is holding above 68%, and our enterprise pilot cohort - eight organizations across healthcare, financial services, and professional services - has produced learner completion rates that are, frankly, jaw-dropping compared to the industry average. These are not vanity metrics. They are evidence that the underlying model is working.

Importantly, the model is this: adaptive learning pathways that respond in real time to how a learner actually engages, not how we expected them to engage. Our secret sauce is the combination of spaced repetition, mastery-based progression, and a content recommendation engine that gets meaningfully smarter with every interaction. That engine is a force multiplier for the instructional design teams at our enterprise customers, empowering them to achieve learning outcomes they could not get with best-of-breed traditional platforms.

It is worth noting that we are still early. There is an enormous amount of work ahead. But this moment - when the data starts confirming the thesis - is the moment I have been working toward for three years.

The future of learning is personalized, intelligent, and relentlessly learner-centered. We are building it. Thank you for believing in us.

- Marcus
