# Keynote Abstract: The Agentic SaaS Era - Building Products for a World Where AI Is the Primary User

We are at the beginning of a transition that will reshape every category of enterprise software: the shift from human-primary to agent-primary product design. In the agentic era, your product will increasingly be consumed not by a person clicking through a UI but by an AI agent executing tasks programmatically through your APIs. The implications for how you build, price, and position SaaS products are profound - and most product leaders are not yet thinking about them.

In this keynote, I will make the case that the SaaS companies that thrive in the next decade will be the ones that recognize this shift early and design for it deliberately. That means API-first architecture that does not treat programmatic access as a second-class citizen. It means pricing models that make sense when usage can scale by 100x overnight as AI workloads ramp. And it means rethinking what "user experience" means when the user is increasingly not human.

Notably, this is not a distant future scenario. The inflection is happening now. Developers at our most sophisticated enterprise customers are already deploying AI agents that interact with SaaS products programmatically at a scale that exceeds what their human teams ever achieved. Importantly, those interactions expose architectural assumptions that were sensible for human users and become liabilities at machine speed and scale.

I will share specific examples of what agent-ready SaaS design looks like in practice: the API design patterns, the permission models, the rate limit architectures, and the webhook designs that create a best-of-breed experience for agentic workloads. And I will talk about the organizational changes that are required to actually ship these capabilities - because the technical decisions and the go-to-market decisions are deeply intertwined.

The product leaders who understand the agentic transition are building the next generation of dominant SaaS companies. This session is for them.
