## 000034-002

The experiment involved the use of three different surface coverings on a fixed slope. A wooden block was released from the same point on each covering, and its travel time was measured.

The angle remained fixed throughout, so the results compare those coverings at one slope rather than describing their behaviour at every possible angle.

## 000034-018

The weighted mean gives more influence to readings that represent longer intervals. This is useful when a sensor records frequently during a disturbance and less frequently afterward. An unweighted average would give the disturbance disproportionate influence simply because it produced more readings.

The calculation assumes each reading represents conditions until the next one. That approximation is reasonable for a slowly changing signal. When the signal changes rapidly between observations, the interval weights alone cannot recover its path, and the sampling schedule becomes part of the interpretation.
