We Started With a Simple Refusal

We refused to believe that the choice between good packaging and responsible packaging had to exist.

Every year, billions of consumer products ship inside materials that will outlast the things they carried by decades, sometimes centuries. The box that held your vitamins will still be here long after the vitamins are gone, long after the shelf they sat on is gone, long after the store has closed. We think that is a solvable problem. Northwind exists to solve it.

---

What We Build

Northwind designs and manufactures sustainable packaging for consumer brands: boxes, wraps, mailers, inserts, and protective materials made from inputs that return to the earth instead of accumulating in it. We work across categories from food and beverage to personal care to direct-to-consumer retail, partnering with brands that want packaging to reflect what they actually stand for.

Our materials are sourced from certified renewable inputs, processed without the petrochemical binders and coatings that make conventional packaging so difficult to break down, and tested to the same structural and protective standards the industry has always required. Sustainability is not a concession we made to conscience. It is the design constraint we started from.

---

Why This Matters Now

The packaging industry produces roughly 150 million metric tons of plastic waste annually. Most of it comes from decisions made in the supply chain before a brand ever considers its carbon commitments. The material specification, the laminate choice, the ink formulation - these are engineering decisions that look invisible to consumers but determine whether a product's packaging can be composted, recycled, or safely broken down.

Consumer brands are under real pressure to close that gap, and most want to. The obstacle has never been intention. It has been access to packaging partners who can match performance, meet timelines, and stay within cost parameters that make sustainable choices commercially viable. Northwind is built to be that partner.

The Team Behind the Work

Northwind was founded by people who spent careers inside large packaging manufacturers watching good sustainability intentions stop at the procurement spreadsheet. We left to build the alternative. Our team includes materials scientists, supply chain specialists, and brand strategists who understand that packaging is not a commodity decision - it is a brand decision with consequences that extend far beyond the shelf.

Our Commitment

We measure our progress by the materials we keep out of landfills, the brands we help transition, and the distance we put between conventional industry practice and what is actually possible. We are not here to greenwash packaging. We are here to replace it.
