A Simple Refusal

Good packaging and responsible packaging can be the same thing. That belief is where Northwind began.

Every year, billions of products ship inside materials that will outlast the things they carried by decades. The box that held your vitamins will still be here long after the vitamins are gone. Long after the store has closed. This is a solvable problem. Northwind exists to solve it.

---

What We Build

Northwind makes sustainable packaging for consumer brands. Boxes, wraps, mailers, inserts, and protective materials. All made from inputs that go back to the earth rather than pile up in it. Our work spans food and beverage, personal care, and retail. We partner with brands that want their packaging to match their values.

Our materials come from certified renewable sources. They are made without the chemical binders that make standard packaging hard to break down. They meet the same strength and safety standards the industry has always required. We built the whole system around sustainability from day one.

---

Why This Matters Now

The packaging industry creates roughly 150 million metric tons of plastic waste each year. Most of it traces back to choices made in the supply chain before a brand ever sets carbon goals. Material specs, laminate choices, ink types - these look invisible to buyers but decide whether packaging can be composted, recycled, or broken down safely.

Brands face real pressure to close that gap. Most want to. The problem has never been will. It has been finding partners who match performance, meet deadlines, and keep costs low enough to make the switch viable. Northwind is built to be that partner.
