# Introducing Lumen: the analytics dashboard that tells you what your data means

For years, business intelligence meant staring at charts and hoping someone in the room had an answer. Today, that changes. We are proud to launch Lumen, our AI-powered analytics dashboard that turns raw data into clear, usable decisions -- on its own.

## See everything. Understand it right away.

Lumen connects to your data sources in minutes -- from CRM and ERP systems to ad platforms and sheets -- and surfaces the insights that drive revenue. No more waiting for an analyst to pull a report. No more dashboards that show numbers without context. Lumen tells you what is happening, why it is happening, and what to do next.

The AI engine monitors your metrics around the clock. When something shifts -- a spike in churn, a drop in conversion, an odd cost trend -- Lumen flags it before it grows and explains it in plain language your whole team can act on.

## Built for teams that move fast

Lumen was built for decision-makers who cannot wait. Whether you run a SaaS company, a retail shop, or a services business, Lumen adapts to your industry, your KPIs, and your workflow.

Key features include:

- **Anomaly detection** -- Lumen watches your data all day and alerts you to changes the moment they appear.
- **Natural language queries** -- Ask any question in plain English and get an answer drawn from your data, with sources shown.
- **Predictive forecasting** -- See where your key metrics are headed over the next 30, 60, and 90 days based on past patterns and live signals.
- **Shared insight feeds** -- Share findings, add context, and assign follow-up actions right inside the dashboard.
