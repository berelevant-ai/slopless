# # Why Industrial IoT Systems Are Starting To Use Wireless RS485 LoRa Communication

Many industrial automation systems still rely on traditional RS485 communication because it is stable, simple, and widely supported by industrial devices.

But once monitoring systems expand across larger facilities, long-distance RS485 cabling can become difficult to manage.

Factories, pump stations, warehouses, and water treatment systems often require communication between devices located far from each other. In these situations, deploying physical cables may increase both installation cost and maintenance complexity.

That is one reason why wireless LoRa communication is becoming more common in Industrial IoT infrastructure.
