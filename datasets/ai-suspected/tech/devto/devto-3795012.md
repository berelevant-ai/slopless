## What Is AI Staff Augmentation?
AI staff augmentation is a hiring model where companies bring external AI professionals into their existing teams.

Instead of outsourcing an entire project, businesses keep control of product development while augmented engineers work alongside internal teams.

This approach helps companies:

- Access AI expertise faster
- Scale teams based on project requirements
- Reduce recruitment delays
- Maintain control over development processes
- Lower long-term hiring commitments

As AI adoption continues to grow, this model has become increasingly popular among startups and enterprises alike.

## Why Companies Are Using AI Staff Augmentation
The demand for AI talent has grown significantly over the last few years. Many organizations face challenges such as:

- Long hiring cycles
- High recruitment costs
- Shortage of experienced AI professionals
- Tight product delivery timelines

AI staff augmentation helps solve these issues by allowing companies to quickly onboard experienced engineers and start building sooner.
