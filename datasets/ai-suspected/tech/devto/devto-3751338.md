# I Built an AI Creative Studio for Video & Image Generation — RenderZero Studio

I’ve been building an AI creative platform called RenderZero Studio over the past few months:

**https://renderzerostudio.com**

The original idea came from one problem I kept running into myself:

Most AI video/image tools feel fragmented.

One platform for text-to-video.
Another for image generation.
Another for cinematic rendering.
Another for editing workflows.

So I decided to build a more unified experience focused on creators, developers, marketers, and startups.

RenderZero Studio combines:

AI video generation
AI image generation
image-to-video workflows
multiple AI models
cinematic-style rendering
fast creative iteration in one dashboard

The goal isn’t just “generate AI content”.

It’s to make the workflow actually usable for production, ads, landing pages, UGC, social content, and rapid experimentation.

A lot of inspiration came from modern AI creative tools and browser-based creative studios that are pushing toward all-in-one workflows instead of isolated generators.

Still shipping updates continuously and improving the platform every week.
