# Stop Paying for Invoice Software These Free Browser Tools Do Everything



I've been freelancing for a couple of years, and for the longest time I was either paying for invoicing software I barely used or downloading clunky templates that broke the moment I opened them on a different machine.

Then I realized: everything I need to bill a client can be done in a browser. No signup, no subscription, no app to install.

Here's the stack I actually use now — and why each one earns its place.

---

## 1. GST Invoice Generator (for Indian freelancers)

If you're billing Indian clients, you know the pain of getting GST formatting right. Wrong CGST/SGST split, missing HSN codes, wrong invoice series — and your client's accountant sends it back.

I use the **[GST Invoice Generator on DocForge](https://www.docforge.website/tools/gst-invoice-generator)** for all domestic Indian billing. It handles:

- CGST + SGST split for intra-state
- IGST for inter-state
- HSN/SAC code fields
- Sequential invoice numbering

There's also a [product-specific GST invoice variant](https://docforge.website/tools/gst-invoice-generator/product) if you're selling physical goods rather than services. Took me 2 minutes to generate a GST-compliant invoice the first time I used it.

If you're a freelancer filing ITR or working with clients who need proper GST invoices for their input tax credit, this one matters.

## 2. Global Invoice Generator

For international clients, GST doesn't apply — but you still need a clean, professional invoice.

The **[Global Invoice Generator](https://docforge.website/tools/invoice-generator/global)** covers country-specific formats. I've used it for clients in:

- **[Canada](https://docforge.website/tools/invoice-generator?country=Canada)** — includes HST/PST fields
- **[UAE](https://www.docforge.website/tools/invoice-generator?country=UAE)** — VAT-ready
- **[Australia](https://www.docforge.website/tools/invoice-generator?country=Australia)** — ABN field included

The professions variants are also solid. I used the **[Developer invoice template](https://www.docforge.website/tools/invoice-generator?prof=Developer)** for a recent contract — it pre-formats the line items in a way that makes sense for hourly dev work.

## 3. Receipt Generator

Invoices are for billing. Receipts are for confirming payment was received.

The **[Receipt Generator](https://www.docforge.website/tools/receipt-generator)** is underrated — most tools conflate the two. This one is specifically for generating payment receipts, which is what you send *after* the invoice is settled.

Super useful for:
- Freelancers who need to provide proof of payment receipt
- Small business owners who deal with cash or offline transactions
- Anyone whose client asks for a receipt to log expenses

## 4. VAT Calculator

Quick one. If you're dealing with European clients and need to figure out VAT-inclusive vs VAT-exclusive pricing before putting it on an invoice, the **[VAT Calculator](https://www.docforge.website/tools/vat-calculator)** is right there.

It supports UK, Germany, and other EU country rates. No conversion needed, just plug in the number and get both the VAT amount and the gross total.

## 5. Proforma Invoice Generator

If a client needs a quote before the work starts — or if you're dealing with customs/import situations — a proforma invoice is what they need, not a standard invoice.

The **[Proforma Invoice Generator](https://docforge.website/tools/proforma-invoice-generator)** handles this exactly. Same fields as a regular invoice but clearly marked as proforma so there's no confusion about whether payment is due.

## 8. Credit Note Generator

Mistakes happen — wrong amount on an invoice, cancelled order, return. A credit note is the formal document that cancels or adjusts a previous invoice.

The **[Credit Note Generator](https://docforge.website/tools/credit-note-generator)** is one of those tools you won't need often, but when you do, you really need it formatted correctly.
