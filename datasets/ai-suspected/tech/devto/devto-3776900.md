The **Technical Students' Society (TSS)** at **Guru Nanak Dev University (GNDU), Amritsar**, is the official student body dedicated to fostering innovation and bridging the gap between academic learning and industry practices. Established with a legacy spanning decades and officially recognized in 2026, TSS actively organizes a variety of events and initiatives to empower students.

**Workshops**: Hands-on sessions to develop technical skills.

**Hackathons**: Events that encourage problem-solving and rapid development.

**Technical Fests**: University-wide events showcasing student talent and innovation.

**Student Initiatives**: Programs designed to support and enhance the student experience.

**Our Teams**
TSS operates with a diverse set of specialized teams, each contributing to the vibrant campus life and operational success of the society:

**Technical**: Manages hackathons, workshops, and event infrastructure.

**Sports**: Organizes inter-college sports competitions.

**Placement**: Facilitates career opportunities by connecting students with recruiters.

**Cultural**: Manages campus fests and memorable events.

**Alumni**: Builds networks and mentorship opportunities between alumni and current students.

**Sponsorship**: Secures partnerships and funding for TSS events.

**Content & Media**: Documents and promotes TSS activities across various channels.

**Design**: Creates the visual identity for TSS, including event creatives and digital experiences.

TSS GNDU welcomes all students enrolled at Guru Nanak Dev University who have a passion for technology, design, or management. Membership is free, focusing on talent and collective growth. Stay updated on upcoming events, competitions, and registration links through the official website and social media channels.
