# From 0 to 70 users in 48 hours — how I built an AI resume roaster

I bought a domain in aug and didn't even use it again.Few days ago I thought to make it meaningful and made a website called roast my resume.

It all started with many of my resumes over more than 50+ applications, not crossing the line or making it the next round.I was really frustrated and had no clue all the websites which were giving ATS scores were still hiding the key points which need to be present.That was major concerning issue.

So, I made a website which has actually been solving this issue It gives an ATS score, the five fix resume solution,five different choices of personalities to roast your resume and download card too. Does not store your resume And also says you are current level like entry,start up, product co, faang.

All these features for free.If your interested, pls look out.It might be helpful for you too.
