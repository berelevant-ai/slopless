# How to Request PromoStandards Credentials Directly Through PSRESTful

PromoStandards credentials are what let distributors connect with suppliers electronically for product data, real-time inventory, pricing, media content, order status, invoices, and more. Every supplier manages their own credentials, so as a distributor you typically have to request them individually from each supplier you work with, by email, through a portal, or over the phone.

  PSRESTful now lets you request credentials from participating suppliers without leaving the platform. You fill in your info once, pick a supplier, and send a complete, professional request in one click. This guide walks through the full process.

  

## Step 1: Set up your Org Info (one time)

  Open the **Credentials** page from your dashboard and switch to the **Request** tab.

  ![PSRESTful dashboard with the user menu open on Credentials, pointing to the Supplier Credentials page where the Request tab sits next to the Configure tab](https://blog.psrestful.com/images/credentials-request-tab.png)

  At the top you'll find the **Org Info** section. This is where you enter the contact details that get attached to every credential request you send: your name, email, and phone number.

  Below your contact info is a **Default Message** field, the actual message the supplier receives when you request credentials. We pre-fill it with a clear, professional request that asks for production credentials and lists the PromoStandards services you'll need, including Product Data, Media Content, Pricing and Configuration, Inventory, and Invoice. You're free to edit this message to add or remove services depending on what you need.
  
  ![PSRESTful Org Info section showing Contact Name, Contact Email, and Contact Phone fields above an editable Default Message that asks the supplier for production PromoStandards credentials](https://blog.psrestful.com/images/credentials-request-org-info.png)

  Set this up once and every future request reuses it.

  

## Step 2: Add your industry identifiers

  Scroll down to the **Industry Identifiers** section. This is where you store your ASI number, SAGE number, PPAI number, UPIC, or any other identifier you use in the promotional products industry. You can add multiple identifiers and mark one as your default so it gets included automatically with every credential request.

  ![PSRESTful Industry Identifiers table listing an ASI Number marked as default, a SAGE Number, and a PPAI Number, with an Add Identifier 
  button](https://blog.psrestful.com/images/credentials-industry-identifiers.png)

  Click **Add Identifier** to pick a type, enter the value, and optionally mark it as your default. The supported identifier types are SAGE Number, ASI Number, PPAI Number, UPIC, and a general **Other** option for anything else.

  ![Add Industry Identifier dialog in PSRESTful with a Type dropdown, a Value field, and a Use as default checkbox](https://blog.psrestful.com/images/credentials-add-identifier.png)

  Having your identifiers attached to the request helps suppliers locate your account quickly and issue credentials faster.

## Step 3: Pick a supplier and send your request
  
  Once your profile is set up, requesting credentials takes seconds. Under the **Request Credentials** section, select a supplier from the dropdown. You can also enter your account number with that supplier, which is optional but helps them find your distributor record faster.

  ![PSRESTful Request Credentials form with a supplier dropdown open on SanMar, an optional account number field, and a Send Request button](https://blog.psrestful.com/images/credentials-request-send.png)

  Click **Send Request** and the supplier receives an email with your contact info, industry identifiers, and your message. Everything they need to issue your PromoStandards credentials arrives in a single, consistent format.

  Your past requests are tracked in the **History** section below the form, so you always have a record of which suppliers you've contacted and when.

  ![PSRESTful History table showing a past credential request to SanMar with a relative timestamp and a Credentials saved status](https://blog.psrestful.com/images/credentials-request-history.png)

## Step 4: Enter your credentials once you receive them
  
  After the supplier issues your credentials, switch over to the **Configure** tab. This is your central dashboard for managing all of your supplier credentials in one place. You can add credentials one at a time, import them in bulk via CSV, or download your full list.

  Each row in the table shows the supplier name, supplier code, the date the credential was created, and the current status of your staging (STG) and production (PROD) credentials. For most suppliers a single set of credentials is all you need, because the supplier serves all of their PromoStandards endpoints through one vendor.

  Some suppliers, though, host different PromoStandards services through more than one vendor. That's what **Show Services** is for: expand a row to see each PromoStandards service for that supplier, such as Product Data, Media Content, Inventory, Pricing and Configuration, Order Status, and Invoice, and override the main credentials on a per-service basis when a particular service lives behind a different vendor with its own login.
  
  If you'd rather skip the request flow entirely because a supplier already gave you credentials, you can add them directly here.

## What are PromoStandards credentials used for?
  
  PromoStandards defines a set of web services that standardize how product data flows between suppliers and distributors. Some of these services, like Product Data and Inventory, can work with shared agent credentials that PSRESTful already has. But services that involve your specific business relationship with a supplier, like your negotiated customer pricing, purchase orders, order status, shipment tracking, and invoices, require credentials issued directly to you.
  
  That's why requesting your own credentials matters. Once you have them configured in PSRESTful, you unlock the full range of PromoStandards services for that supplier, all accessible through a single REST API.
