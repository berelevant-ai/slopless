# ClassPilot 1st Anniversary

Hey again everyone,

Its me again, and exactly one year ago today, I released **ClassPilot**.
I originally built it because I was genuinely struggling to handle my own schedule. Between classes, projects, and life, everything was chaotic. I needed a tool that was built by a student, for students to solve one core problem: Time Management.

The development journey over the last year took months of late-night coding, learning from mistakes, and endless debugging. But seeing other students actually use the app to pull their grades up and get their schedules under control has been the most rewarding experience of my life.
**What is ClassPilot?** It’s an intelligent academic tracker designed to help you stay on top of your classes with a massive focus on productivity. It tracks grades, schedules, and gives you a beautiful, distraction-free environment to work in.

**Coming Soon (v2.0.5)** This June marks a huge milestone for the app, but also a bittersweet one. Version 2.0.5 will be the final major feature update to be added on ClassPilot.

For this grand finale, I’m releasing something I’m incredibly proud of: an **offline AI Chatbot system**. It’s designed to run entirely locally on your device. I believe that as long as curiosity drives you, learning should never stop, even if you don’t have an internet connection this would be very helpful to an area with poor internet connection especially here in Philippines.

To the dedicated users who have supported this app from the very beginning: it would be a pleasure for your continuous support. This app stands as proof that _meaningful impact isn't defined by the resources we're given, but by the purpose we choose to serve_.
