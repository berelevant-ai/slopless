# 5 Better Alternatives to Bitly You Should Try in 2026

Bitly dominated URL shortening for years, but in 2026 it's no longer the obvious choice. Rate limits, short analytics retention windows, and paywalled features have pushed developers and marketers toward more capable tools. This guide breaks down five solid alternatives - what they do best, who they're for, and where they fall short.

## Quick Comparison

| Service | Best For | Free Tier | Standout Feature |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **DevToolLab URL Tracker** | Developers and marketers | Excellent - 1-year data retention | Unlimited premium links, custom slugs free |
| **Rebrandly** | Brand-focused teams | Moderate | Multi-domain management + retargeting pixels |
| **Short.io** | Developers needing API control | Good - custom domains included | Dynamic routing by OS/device |
| **Cuttly** | Solo creators and small agencies | Moderate | QR codes + link-in-bio built in |
| **TinyURL** | Quick, no-fuss shortening | Basic | No account needed, links never expire |

## 1. DevToolLab URL Tracker

If Bitly's analytics paywall frustrates you, DevToolLab's [URL Tracker](https://devtoollab.com/tools/url-tracker) was built as a direct response. Free users get 5 tracked links with up to 1,000 clicks/month - and crucially, analytics are retained for a full year rather than being wiped at 30 days.

Even on the free plan you get custom slugs, password protection, link expiration, click limits, and UTM tracking. Upgrading removes all caps for unlimited tracked links. It plugs neatly into the rest of DevToolLab's developer tools (webhooks, JSON formatters, Base64 utilities) from a single dashboard.

**Best pick if:** you want honest, long-term analytics without paying for features that should be standard.

## 2. Rebrandly

[Rebrandly](https://rebrandly.com/) treats branded domains as the core product, not an upsell. You can connect multiple custom domains and assign them to separate projects or sub-brands. A standout feature is the ability to fire retargeting pixels (Facebook, Google, LinkedIn) directly from the short link itself - before the user even reaches your landing page.

Team workflows are well thought out, with clean permission management across multiple users touching the same campaigns.

**Best pick if:** brand trust and retargeting are non-negotiable for your campaigns.

## 3. Short.io

[Short.io](https://short.io/) is built for programmatic link management. Its API is clean and well-documented, integrations with Zapier/Make/Slack work without friction, and the platform supports dynamic routing - send iOS users to the App Store and Android users to Google Play from the same link.

Unlike most competitors, Short.io includes custom domains on its free tier, which alone makes it worth evaluating for any developer running multiple projects.

**Best pick if:** you need API-driven link creation and device-based traffic routing.

## 4. Cuttly

[Cuttly](https://cutt.ly/) bundles URL shortening, branded QR code generation, link-in-bio pages, and A/B split testing into one dashboard. It's the kind of tool that eliminates four separate subscriptions for a solo dev or small agency.

The QR codes are fully branded and don't expire with the link - a common annoyance with third-party generators. A/B testing lets you split traffic between two destinations from a single short URL.

**Best pick if:** you want an all-in-one stack and don't want to juggle multiple tools.

## 5. TinyURL

[TinyURL](https://tinyurl.com/) hasn't changed much, and that's the point. No account needed - paste a long URL and get a short one in seconds. You can set a custom text alias even without signing up, and the links historically never expire.

There's no analytics depth, no API power, no retargeting. But for dropping a clean link in a Slack message or a quick share, nothing is faster.

**Best pick if:** you just need a short link right now with zero friction.

## Which One Should You Use?

The right tool depends on what you're actually building:

- **Long-term analytics + developer integration** - DevToolLab URL Tracker
- **Multi-brand campaigns with retargeting** - Rebrandly
- **Backend automation and device routing** - Short.io
- **All-in-one creative toolkit** - Cuttly
- **Instant, zero-setup shortening** - TinyURL

All five are meaningfully better than Bitly in at least one dimension. Start with what matches your current bottleneck and scale from there.
