# Built a simple free printable calendar tool — nothing fancy

A few months back I found myself spending way too long just trying to format a monthly calendar in Word so I could print it. It felt silly. There had to be a simpler way, so I just built one.

It's called Online Timezone Calendar. You pick a month and year, and it gives you a clean printable calendar right away. No account, no payment, nothing to download. Just open it and print.

[Try it here](https://www.onlinetimezone.com/calendar/create-monthly.html)

Most calendar tools I found online were either too complicated or kept asking me to sign up just to get a basic PDF. I didn't want any of that. I just wanted something that works in seconds.

Building it taught me a few things. Simple tools still matter. SEO for utility pages is very specific, people search for things like "printable calendar June 2026" not just "calendar". And honestly, getting real users is harder than building the thing itself.

I'm planning to add more calendar types and holiday support down the road. Still a work in progress.

If you've built something similar or have feedback after trying it, I'd genuinely love to hear from you.
