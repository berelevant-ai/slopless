# I analyzed 1,000 search queries hitting my free tools site after 4 weeks — here is what I learned

I launched TrendPro (trendproservices.co.uk) a few weeks ago — a free
browser-based tools site with 86+ utilities for developers, writers and
students.

After 4 weeks I checked Google Search Console for the first time. The
data was both surprising and instructive. Here is what I found.

## The numbers

- 2,770 impressions in 28 days
- 7 clicks total (0.25% CTR)
- 1,000 different search queries showing my pages
- Average position: 47

## What was actually ranking

The tools that got the most impressions were not the ones I expected:

**BMI Calculator — 114 impressions**
People searching "calculate bmi" are finding my calculator. But 0 clicks.
My title was too generic. I am rewriting it to show the actual outputs
upfront: health category, ideal weight range, how much to lose or gain.

**Meta Tag Generator — 76 impressions**
Two separate keyword variants hitting the same page. 0 clicks.
Title and description were not specific enough about what the tool
actually outputs (title tag, description, OG tags, Twitter Card tags all
at once in one HTML block).

**Remove Duplicate Lines — 30 impressions, 1 click, position 17**
This is my best opportunity right now. Position 17 means I am at the
top of page 2. A better title, a blog post on the topic and a few
backlinks should push this to page 1.

**Password Generator — 67 impressions, 0 clicks, position ~7.5**
Position 7 is on page 1 of Google. But 0 clicks. The issue is the
search query is "how to let google generate a strong password" — an
informational query — but my page is a tool page. There is an intent
mismatch. I need a blog post that answers the question AND links to
the tool.

## The key insight

High impressions + zero clicks = wrong title/description, not wrong tool.

Google is showing your pages because the content matches the keywords.
But if the snippet (title + description) does not immediately answer what
the searcher wants, they scroll past.

The fix is not to change the tool — it is to change the 160 characters
Google shows in the results.
