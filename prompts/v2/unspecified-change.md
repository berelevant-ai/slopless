Find a change, transition, improvement, worsening, or change in difficulty that is announced while what changed remains unspecified. YES: **Something had shifted. The work felt different now.** NO: The review changed from a weekly meeting to written comments on each release. Surrounding sentences or a clear before-and-after comparison can supply the change implicitly. Details of a meeting, person, time, or location alone do not identify a change. Preserve a stated qualitative change even without a numerical measure. When the sole missing information is the coverage of a narrow or bounded task, that belongs to unspecified scope. Missing consequences of importance, costs, or risks belong to hollow significance. Stock atmospheric scene description belongs to narrative craft.
