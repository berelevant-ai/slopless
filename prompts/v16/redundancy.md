# Redundancy

Find information expressed twice within a phrase or adjacent statements, including a heading and its opening. Identify the duplicated meaning and test the function of its second expression. Count a duplicate that adds no relevant distinction or useful communicative role, including one beside useful new information.

Cover repeated modifiers, equivalent propositions in different words, doubled timing or approximation, and label/body or heading/opening restatement. Judge absolute modifiers in context: a modifier that merely repeats completeness can be redundant, while comparison between incomplete drafts can be meaningful.

Preserve useful plain-language glosses, unit conversions, additional events, scope restrictions, degree, confirmations, and actionable instructions. A heading can orient the reader while an opening performs the first step of a usable procedure. Apparent repetition can enforce an exception or distinguish what is possible from what is available now.

Repeated substantive explanations after intervening content belong to repeated explanation. An apparent reason that only restates its claim belongs to circular explanation. Wordiness requires needless elaboration of one point; this check requires duplicated information. Assess quoted wording the writer adopts, and preserve speech or wording recorded for examination or documentation.

## Hits

### Example 1

> I've applied the credit for the missing item. The **remaining balance still left** on your account is $18.40, which will be collected on your usual billing date.
>
> You can see the adjustment under Orders, then Payment history. If the credit hasn't appeared there by tomorrow morning, reply to this message and I'll check the posting.

### Example 2

> The error rate crossed the paging threshold at 09:08. **The alert reached the on-call engineer at 09:14, six minutes later.** **There was a six-minute gap between crossing the threshold and notifying the engineer.**
>
> The notification service had queued the page behind a batch of routine messages. We moved incident pages to a separate queue during recovery and checked that a test page reached the on-call phone.

### Example 3

> Your address update is **fully complete**. Both the order and the delivery label now show 28 Lake Road. The courier will send a new tracking link this evening.

## No-Hits

### Example 1

> The cushion cover can feel dry while the foam inside is still damp. Press a clean towel firmly against the centre of the insert; a wet mark means it needs more time on the drying rack.
>
> Wait until the insert is completely dry before putting the cover back on. The zipped cover slows the escape of moisture from the foam.

### Example 2

> The replacement strap is available in navy. We have the navy version in stock. I can send it today if you confirm the buckle width; the photo shows the narrow fitting, which comes in two sizes.

### Example 3

> The repair work is due to finish in roughly a fortnight, about two weeks. During that period, use the path beside the tennis courts to reach the allotments. The gate there will be unlocked from dawn until dusk.
