# Wordiness

Read each suspected expression literally in the full passage before proposing an edit. Identify what its words contribute. Require both conditions for a hit:

- A specific shorter wording preserves the claim, actor, qualifications, timing and useful emphasis.
- The original contains identifiable padding or an unnecessarily elaborate construction. An available shorter synonym or equally good paraphrase satisfies only the first condition.

Distinguish direct ordinary formal wording from a detour through generic scaffolding around the point. Judge the detour, rather than the word count or grammatical category. A noun or auxiliary can name something the passage discusses or distinguishes; reducing it to the nearest verb may collapse that meaning. Preserve what the writer asserts, rather than merely its practical consequence.

Ordinary useful formal wording needs no special technical justification. Check its function before calling it padding. A nominalized action with only generic performance scaffolding can become an actor-neutral passive verb. This preserves an unspecified actor while removing the detour. Apply the same test across unfamiliar constructions, including elaborate identity and location expressions.

Preserve wording being documented, translated or explained; assess surrounding assertions independently. Judge wording adopted by the writer normally. Informative details leave independent padding intact. Duplicated information belongs to redundancy; a disposable announcement around a supplied point belongs to unnecessary framing.

## Paired Examples

### Relation Versus Scaffolding

#### Hit

> Suppose twenty classes are assigned by lottery to use one of two textbooks. We compare their average test scores at the end of term. The difference between the two group averages **is what constitutes** our estimate of the textbook's effect.
>
> The lottery assigns whole classes rather than individual pupils, so pupils in the same class share both a teacher and a treatment. The uncertainty calculation must account for that grouping; treating every pupil as an independent observation would make the estimate appear more precise than it is.

#### No-Hit

> Northbank's scanner has the capability to read labels through the clear sleeves we use on returned boxes. The less expensive model needed the sleeve removed in four of our twenty trials. Both connected to the stock system without an adapter.
>
> For the returns bench, I'd choose Northbank. Removing sleeves slowed the test down, and we would need a bin beside the desk for the discarded plastic. The cheaper scanner would still suit the dispatch station, where the labels are exposed.

The hit wraps an identity in extra scaffolding. The no-hit directly states a useful capability; a shorter modal paraphrase alone establishes no padding.

### Action Versus Referenced Output

#### Hit

> **The replacement of expired keys will now be performed** automatically during the overnight maintenance window. Active connections keep their existing key until they reconnect. Administrators receive a summary of replacements at the address configured in Security Settings.

#### No-Hit

> The fixed-price proposal includes removal of the old tank. The other supplier will make an assessment of access before quoting for removal, because its smaller crane may need a temporary position in the neighbour's yard. We should obtain that assessment before comparing the delivered prices.

The hit's performance frame can become a passive verb. The no-hit's assessment names an output to obtain and use; its noun construction serves that meaning.

### Cause Versus Fact Wrapper

#### Hit

> **Due to the fact that** the loading bay will be resurfaced, deliveries need to use the front entrance next week. Small parcels can come through reception. Pallets will need a timed slot so the driver can unload without blocking the pavement.
>
> I've put the temporary unloading schedule in the shared folder. Please add any expected pallets by Thursday lunchtime.

#### No-Hit

> The coach arrived late as a result of a lorry breaking down on the bypass. We waited in the school office, where the secretary found paper cups for the tea. By the time the driver called, the rain had stopped and everyone was outside checking which cases belonged to whom.

The hit adds a generic fact wrapper around its cause. The no-hit uses an ordinary causal expression; a shorter connector alone establishes no defect.
