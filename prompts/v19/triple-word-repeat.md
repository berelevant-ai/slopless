# Triple Word Repeat

Judge formulaic enumeration, despite this legacy check's name. Find one coordinated list of three or more members whose interchangeable rhetorical roles manufacture completeness.

- Cover evaluative adjectives, abstract nouns and parallel rhetorical verbs, including unseen words and lists longer than three. The members need not be literal synonyms.
- Identify a defect in packaging the list, independently of whether a claim is exaggerated, true or sufficiently explained. Generic praise alone belongs to the existing evaluation check; missing substance alone belongs to semantic thinness.
- Preserve useful distinct properties, criteria, objects and operations. Three members alone establish no defect. A useful list remains a no-hit here even if one individual claim deserves another check.
- Useful surrounding facts do not repair an independent ornamental triad. Conversely, a weak claim does not make its enumeration defective automatically.
- Judge the supplied meaning rather than membership in an adjective or noun vocabulary.
- Preserve lists reproduced for analysis, correction or documentation. Assess adopted quotations and ordinary dialogue by the same contribution test.

Require one coordinated enumeration, such as members sharing a clause or an actual list structure. Three separate promotional sentences are fragment or frame material, not this list check. Do not convert nearby action narration or an inherited-fragment stack into a coordinated triad. Preserve distinct operations with different objects or effects. Quoted copy that is deleted, criticized, compared or documented stays outside adopted rhetoric; mere quotation marks around adopted advertising offer no exemption.

The side pocket takes my large water bottle without straining the zipper, and I can reach my keys without taking the bag off. After a month the lining still looks clean. One shoulder strap has started to squeak at the top seam, but I have kept using it every day.

Please keep children out of the fenced area until the opening notice appears. The engineer must test the drain covers with the pool full before it can reopen.
