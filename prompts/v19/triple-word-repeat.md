# Triple Word Repeat

Judge formulaic enumeration, despite this legacy check's name. Find one coordinated list of three or more members whose interchangeable rhetorical roles manufacture completeness.

- Cover evaluative adjectives, abstract nouns and parallel rhetorical verbs, including unseen words and lists longer than three. The members need not be literal synonyms.
- Identify a defect in packaging the list, independently of whether a claim is exaggerated, true or sufficiently explained. Generic praise alone belongs to the existing evaluation check; missing substance alone belongs to semantic thinness.
- Preserve useful distinct properties, criteria, objects and operations. Three members alone establish no defect. A useful list remains a no-hit here even if one individual claim deserves another check.
- Useful surrounding facts do not repair an independent ornamental triad. Conversely, a weak claim does not make its enumeration defective automatically.
- Judge the supplied meaning rather than membership in an adjective or noun vocabulary.
- Preserve lists reproduced for analysis, correction or documentation. Assess adopted quotations and ordinary dialogue by the same contribution test.

Locate the actual comma/conjunction list before assessing its meaning. Three fragments each ending in a period do not become that list. An action sequence is also a different construction. Next ask whether the members serve interchangeable generic rhetorical roles, rather than distinct jobs, physical properties or operations. One vague or exaggerated item among useful distinctions does not establish an independent list-packaging defect. A passage may contain bad fragment beats alongside a completely useful real enumeration; judge only the latter here. A review can quote a manufacturer's praise to contrast it with the reviewer's concrete experience.

## Hits

This backpack has been **fantastic, brilliant, and just plain great** for my walk to the office. The side pocket takes my large water bottle without straining the zipper, and I can reach my keys without taking the bag off. After a month the lining still looks clean. One shoulder strap has started to squeak at the top seam, but I have kept using it every day.

Your membership should help you **thrive, flourish, and reach new heights**. We can transfer your swimming lessons to the evening group when its current course ends. The instructor has accepted your existing assessment. Reply before Monday if you would like me to reserve one of the two remaining places.

The restored paddling pool will be **outstanding, exceptional, impressive, and magnificent**. Filling begins on Monday after the new surface has cured. Please keep children out of the fenced area until the opening notice appears. The engineer must test the drain covers with the pool full before it can reopen.
