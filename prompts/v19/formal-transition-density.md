# Formal Transition Density

Find at least three nearby sentence-prefacing connectors or emphasis markers that repeatedly announce a relationship or significance the passage fails to develop. Generalize beyond any fixed vocabulary.

- Count the complete local sequence, including adjacent paragraphs that continue it. Separate topics, sections, records or dialogue turns establish separate sequences unless explicitly connected.
- Judge what each marker contributes: contrast, consequence, addition, sequence, comparison or emphasis. Frequent formal wording alone establishes no defect.
- Preserve useful reasoning, chronology and comparison at the same frequency. Several markers can be necessary when each communicates a distinct relevant relation.
- Flag staged progression or significance when the announced relations add no useful organization, even if useful details appear elsewhere in the passage.
- Connector-only repetition belongs here; do not infer a separately defective lexical sentence frame merely from the same connectors.
- Preserve wording documented, compared or corrected. Assess adopted quotations and ordinary dialogue by the same contribution test.

Judge the relation each marker expresses between its linked statements, independently of any repeated opening or ending elsewhere. A useful contrast followed by a consequence and an alternative is useful organization at a frequency of three. Other sloppy wording cannot turn those relations into empty transitions. Preserve a message or slogan reproduced for investigation, editing or criticism; assess newly adopted claims normally. No vocabulary item is automatically a hit.

## Hits

The book exchange will have a children's shelf this month. **Moreover, children's books will have a shelf of their own.** Volunteers will put picture books at the low end so children can reach them without climbing. **Additionally, there will be a separate shelf for children's titles.** Please leave school textbooks on the marked table instead, since those are being collected for another group. **Overall, a dedicated children's shelf is part of the exchange.**

The return label expires at the end of this month. **Therefore, it will cease to be usable when the month ends.** **Consequently, next month it will no longer be valid.** **In conclusion, the label's validity ends with this month.** If you cannot reach the parcel shop before then, reply here for a new label.

A local copy lets the application display the last saved schedule without a network connection. **Notably, this offers useful flexibility.** **Crucially, it supports a convenient experience.** **Indeed, convenience is an important consideration.** Changes made while offline are stored on the device and sent to the server after the connection returns.

## No-Hits

The new badge shows that a message has been sent from your device. Importantly, it differs from the existing badge showing that the recipient has read it. Therefore, a sent badge can appear while the recipient is offline. In addition, the message details now spell out both times so the two events can be distinguished without relying on colour.

We began confirming delivery access before loading large cabinets. Consequently, the driver now knows whether a stair lift or an extra person is needed.

However, customers sometimes describe only the entrance and forget an internal step. Therefore, our confirmation message now asks separately about the route from the street and the route inside the building.

The message you forwarded reads, "Accordingly, we care about your order. Thus, your order matters to us. In conclusion, it deserves our care." It gives no dispatch date. I am using the order number beneath that wording to ask the warehouse for a date, and I will reply here when they confirm one.
