# Repeated Predicate End

Find at least three nearby statements ending in the same generic predicate. Include unseen predicates and ordinary inflected variants when the repeated verbal ending performs the same function.

- The generic predicate must recur at statement ends; a repeated word somewhere in each sentence is insufficient. A repeated modal-plus-predicate ending can qualify.
- Judge the contribution of the recurring ending across the complete sequence. Flag a generic repeated beat that substitutes for informative distinctions. Useful information in the subjects can coexist with a separate mechanical ending.
- Preserve useful repeated test results, findings, parallel comparisons, procedural outcomes and deliberate refrain when the writing supplies that function. Numbers, concrete nouns and technical subject matter alone supply no exemption.
- Count one connected local sequence, including adjacent paragraphs continuing it. Separate records, sections and dialogue turns establish separate sequences unless explicitly connected.
- Give the terminal-predicate defect this assignment; assess an independently repeated opening separately.
- Preserve documentary or examined quotations. Assess adopted slogans and ordinary dialogue by the same contribution test.

Require the actual ending to recur; synonymous conclusions with different last words cannot meet this form. Then distinguish redundant generic commentary from useful sameness of result. Different payment routes, tested inputs, observation times or assigned duties may properly share an identical outcome: the common ending makes the comparison or completed work clear. An outcome need not differ to be informative. Other vague promises or transition padding elsewhere do not cancel that function.
