# Flat Action Cadence

Find runs of at least three complete subject-first action clauses presented as equally detached beats. Changed actors, objects and verbs can preserve the same mechanical rhythm.

- Include actor movements, bodily actions, physical scene subjects and linking scene observations. Scene-only sequences can qualify. Independent clauses joined within a sentence can participate.
- Require complete clauses with their own subjects. A bare fragment stack or subject inherited from an earlier sentence belongs to fragment stacking.
- Judge the repeated presentation of the sequence. Distinct actions or concrete plot information do not by themselves supply varied emphasis. Chronological order alone establishes no useful-function exemption.
- Preserve equal short beats when their order or separate emphasis serves a recoverable procedure, event reconstruction, parallel comparison or deliberate pacing effect. A fiction genre, a technical topic or the word footage alone establishes none of those functions.
- Read the complete supplied context, including adjacent paragraphs continuing the sequence. Separate records or unrelated sections establish separate sequences.
- Judge cadence rather than whether each gesture advances the plot. Preserve examined or documentary quotations; judge ordinary narration and dialogue by the same functional test.

Each coordinated clause with its own subject counts, so two sentences may supply four beats. Judge the exact action run independently of any promotional fragments or evaluative list elsewhere. Preserve separate observations that establish which event happened, explain an operational dependency, distinguish tested alternatives or trigger the observer's next decision. Equal observations can deliberately build toward a last required signal or consequence. A mere chronological setting gives no exemption; the writing must supply that function. Incidental bag-lifting, chair-moving or animation choreography can remain gratuitous beside a useful product comparison.

## Hits

The temporary workspace is ready for anyone displaced by the painting upstairs. **The lamps glow along the wall.** **The blinds stay half open.** **A fan turns near the window.** **The room remains cool.** **A curtain moves above the sink.** There are six desks available; leave the one beside the telephone free for the duty coordinator.

I used the new coffee grinder on Sunday morning. **I pulled the bag closer.** **I scooped the beans.** **I touched the lid.** **I turned the dial.** **I pressed the switch.** The grind was even enough for my filter brewer, but the container scattered grounds onto the counter whenever I removed it from the base.

I tried the two grocery delivery services during a quiet afternoon. **The first driver rang the bell, and the second driver knocked on the frame.** **I lifted one bag, and my partner carried the other.** **I shut the door.** Both orders contained everything requested, although the second service charged less for the same delivery window.

## No-Hits

The staffed service receives your calls. Finds the right person. Keeps business flowing. Its routing can be compared with the automated option using one test call. The receptionist asks the department. The caller gives an extension. The switchboard connects the line. A transcript of those steps reveals whether the caller had to know the extension already. Call duration, waiting time, and destination are reported separately.

The providers handle the same failed payment differently. Service A retries the charge. Service B pauses the subscription. Service C emails an invoice. These separate actions determine which provider fits a business that ships physical orders: a retry preserves automatic fulfillment, while a pause or an invoice requires someone to release the next shipment.

The loading camera settles which pallet needs to be recounted. Luis loads the blue pallet, and the driver signs its sheet. Ren loads the red pallet, and the driver leaves the sheet unsigned. The missing signature belongs to the red load. Please reconcile that count with the receiver before closing the transfer rather than reopening both loads.
