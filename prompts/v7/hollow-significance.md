# Hollow Significance

Find every claim of practical importance, impact, benefit, risk, cost, or measurement whose consequence or measured meaning remains unspecified. Include indirect claims about exposure, stakes, a price, dividends, implications, or something having a bearing on a decision.

First identify the passage's own assertion. Return NO for an editor or teacher criticizing significance wording, even when the passage reproduces the bad claim. Preserve technical terms and names containing words such as impact or value. Praise for an artistic achievement, wisdom, a lesson, or a conclusion belongs to puffery or empty takeaway unless a separate practical effect is claimed. A character feeling pressure, fear, or stakes belongs to narrative atmosphere; judge an actual consequence of a choice here.

Read the entire passage for the consequence: what someone can do, gains, loses, pays, risks, or experiences. Preserve a stated or clearly implied practical consequence, including information before the importance claim. A qualitative effect suffices; a number is optional. Merely naming a choice, change, time, location, or affected group leaves its consequence unspecified.

A needed resource, extra procedure, preserved ability, or lost opportunity can supply the complete consequence. Stating the resource or effect is enough; the passage need not also price it or explain its wider significance.

Describing a feature or a distinction alone can leave its practical payoff unspecified. Preserve an account of what that feature lets someone do or what follows from it, rather than requiring further importance beyond the stated effect.

- YES: **Choosing the vendor creates exposure for us.** Its proposal came in a green folder. The possible harm remains unspecified.
- YES: **Waiting a day has a price.** She folded the tickets. The cost remains unspecified.
- NO: The printed symbols let customers distinguish cables without relying on their colors. That is why they matter. The practical benefit is supplied.
- NO: Weekend opening has a cost: it requires a paid supervisor. The labor cost is identified without an amount.
- NO: The spare label matters if the ink on the original fades: its text stays readable. The preserved information supplies the effect.

For measurement, preserve any interpretable metric, value, scale, or calculation. A defined count or method is enough even when the final number is omitted. A description of an instrument and its units is informative without a current reading. Naming a report, table, or result as numerical supplies none of this by itself.

- NO: We measured disruption by counting cancelled sessions. The count is a defined metric.
- NO: The thermometer measures temperature in degrees Celsius. Its current reading is omitted. This describes an instrument.
- YES: **The assessment assigns a number to the market's impact.** It was written last spring. The metric, value, scale, and calculation remain unspecified.

Judge each claim separately. An explained practical benefit can coexist with a separate empty claim that a wider contribution has been quantified. Incidental figures, identifiers, and unrelated details leave that claim unresolved.

Preserve editorial criticism of significance wording. Keep empty lessons and valuable-sounding conclusions under empty takeaway, inflated praise under puffery, and emotional atmosphere under narrative craft.

- NO: The judge called the exhibit a sublime achievement. This is praise.
