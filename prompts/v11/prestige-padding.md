# Prestige Padding

Does the writer or narrator use reputation, credentials, experience, famous associations, media attention, or audience size as support for a claim beyond what that status establishes? Find every such inference, including implicit links across sentences and unfamiliar wording.

Identify the status and the conclusion it is meant to support. Catch unrelated credentials lending weight to advice, fame standing in for reliability, and relevant expertise treated as conclusive proof. The inference can be implied by a recommendation placed beside a status claim; a word such as "therefore" is optional. Irrelevant factual detail leaves that misuse intact.

Preserve qualifications establishing relevant expertise or eligibility, first-hand experience supporting a bounded observation, and follower counts or media mentions explaining reach or recognition. Relevant advice need not be accompanied by a research paper. Biography, appointments, awards, and credits are no-hits when they describe background rather than establish an unrelated or conclusive claim.

Judge whose inference the passage advances. Clearly documenting, examining, or rejecting someone else's authority appeal is a no-hit; criticism is optional for documentation. A fictional character's limited belief remains the character's belief unless the passage adopts it. Ordinary attribution alone leaves an operative recommendation eligible, and the writer can adopt another person's claim explicitly or implicitly.

Judge the relevance and strength of the support presented. Exaggerated praise by itself belongs to inflated evaluation; unidentified expert opinion belongs to vague attribution.

## Hits

- "**A distinguished barrister designed this desk; it should prevent repetitive strain injuries.**" Legal distinction supplies the supposed ergonomic support.
- "Four decades in corporate accounting give Helen a useful perspective on cash flow. **They also settle the question of whether the exchange's reserves are sufficient.** **We can publish her reassuring statement as confirmation of solvency** and leave the balance-sheet review out of this month's investment circular, which goes to 1,200 clients." Relevant experience is extended into conclusive verification.
- "The brochure places the developer's friendship with a famous astronaut beside its claim of fire-safe cladding. **That connection should give buyers confidence when reserving one of the upper-floor flats.**" The writer adopts the irrelevant association as reassurance.

## No-Hits

- "Dr. Bell's clinical experience supports her advice on using the inhaler safely between appointments." The expertise supports advice within its relevant scope.
- "Our caterer has cooked for the royal household. The programme biography includes that appointment among her previous jobs, alongside a photograph from her first community kitchen in Leeds." This describes her background.
- "The release note reports that the SDK will preserve compatibility because its maintainer holds a distinguished fellowship. The first migration window is scheduled for Friday at 18:00." This documents the release note's rationale.
