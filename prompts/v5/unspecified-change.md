# Unspecified Change

Find every announcement of a change whose actual content remains unspecified across the supplied passage. Apply these boundaries before deciding YES.

1. Identify what the passage is asserting. Return NO for criticism of vague writing, including a passage that states a vague claim and then asks its author to explain or replace it. Open questions, denied changes, wishes, and undecided possibilities are also NO.
2. Keep the named check distinct. A smaller or more restricted scope, remit, mandate, brief, or question belongs to unspecified scope and is NO here, including figurative restriction. Stock scene-setting atmosphere belongs to narrative craft. A passing adjective such as "revised" in a claim about importance is NO here.
3. Read all sentences for the actual difference, including information before the announcement. If a changed behavior, procedure, ability, topic, or observable property is stated or clearly inferable, return NO. A broad summary of a supplied concrete difference is resolved by that difference; it needs no additional change. A qualitative difference suffices; a number or formal before-and-after sentence is optional.

- NO: The intern once needed help using the microscope; now she can focus it herself. Training has become easier. The changed ability is supplied.
- NO: He formerly borrowed my tools. Now he lends me his. Our relationship has changed. The reversal is supplied.
- NO: The import became easier: saved column mappings replaced manually choosing each field. The changed work is supplied.
- NO: This draft calls the rollout a transformation, then asks its author to specify the transformation. The passage critiques wording.
- NO: The briefing switches from taxes to workplace safety. The discussion has changed direction. The new topic is supplied.
- NO: The remit has been cut down. A restricted brief belongs to the scope check.

Return YES for an actual change announcement whose difference remains empty: an evolved relationship, revised method, new phase, improvement, deterioration, or work becoming easier, harder, simpler, or more demanding. Knowing only its subject, direction, trigger, or date leaves the change unspecified.

- YES: **The onboarding became less arduous.** Six people attended the training. What difficulty disappeared remains unspecified.
- YES: **Our collaboration took a different form.** We signed the agreement beside the airport. Its new form remains unspecified.

Find equivalent constructions and all separate instances. A resolved change elsewhere leaves an independent empty announcement reportable. Ordinary quoted claims receive the same test as narration.
