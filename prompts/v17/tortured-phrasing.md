# Tortured Phrasing

Find garbled lexical substitutions in conventional names, technical terms and adopted sayings. Require enough context to identify the intended expression and a specific correction. Detect unseen substitutions, not just known examples.

A recognizable intended concept can still have a corrupted name. Distinguish using substituted words as that name or fixed saying from describing an ordinary function or relationship. A synonym alone proves neither corruption nor validity. Preserve established alternatives, ordinary paraphrases, literal descriptions, purposeful wordplay and deliberately invented concepts.

Fictional settings supply the same test: an ordinary technical term can still be garbled inside a story. Preserve wording quoted for translation, correction or examination; judge adopted wording normally. Ordinary grammar errors or malapropisms alone are insufficient. Scientific claims requiring outside verification belong to the editor. Decorative but intact technical terms belong to borrowed jargon.

## Development Examples

### Hit

The school garden stall will sell **costless scope** eggs on Friday afternoon. The hens spend their days outdoors in the orchard and return to their house at dusk. Boxes of six cost two pounds, with the proceeds used to buy feed and repair the orchard fence.

### Hit

Signing the **alphabet character of intent** let us record the proposed purchase terms before the lawyers drafted the final agreement. It identified the price range, review period, and documents we still needed. We marked the provisions requiring immediate commitments separately so both teams understood what had already been agreed.

### No-Hit

The old manual calls the configuration folder a 'settings wardrobe'. Replace that translation with 'configuration directory' in the new edition. Keep the path examples unchanged, since scripts in existing installations still refer to those names and the release does not move any files.

### No-Hit

The service-level agreement covers response time and attendance, but neither supplier guarantees a repair within that period. Oak keeps common parts locally, while Finch orders them after diagnosis. We need to compare likely repair delays as well as the time promised for the first technician's visit.
