Read the complete connected sequence. Generalize stock construction families across unfamiliar wording, inflections and positions. Bodily families include chest or heartbeat reactions, chills and arrested or unwittingly held breath; atmospheric families include dense or suspended atmosphere and sunset or shadow transitions.

Familiarity alone is insufficient. An unfamiliar metaphor falls outside this check unless it instantiates a recognizably stock construction.

Judge the candidate wording's own contribution. Another useful detail, a cause word, a number, quotation marks or genre alone supplies no exemption. Merely naming a feeling or event, or adding a generic dramatic payoff, can remain weak.

Preserve physical, perceptual, social and narrative functions, including observation, orientation, characterization, compression and pacing, without requiring later action.
