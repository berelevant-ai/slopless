# Silence as Actor

Input: passage.

Read the complete connected sequence. Decide this check independently: another defect can coexist, and another check's applicability establishes neither a hit nor a no-hit here.

First locate an eligible absence, pause or place-state cue. Generalize across wording, inflections and positions: the scene can be lively, and silence, an abstract grammatical subject and intentional agency are optional. Claims about a place's generic activity or emotional condition belong to this check when they supply the same thin atmosphere. A human cause can establish how the state arose while leaving its emotional annotation generic.

Then identify what the cue itself contributes. Naming a feeling or giving a setting a social attitude can substitute for particular conditions or interpersonal behavior. Nearby concrete actions, even actions consistent with that attitude, need not make the annotation informative. Evaluate its added meaning rather than transferring the surrounding scene's usefulness to it. One eligible weak cue is enough.

Preserve a cue that itself supplies a particular physical condition, perception, interpersonal signal or narrative relation. Specific social meaning comes from the relationship established between participants, rather than an attitude assigned to the setting. Observation, orientation, characterization, compression and pacing can supply a function without later action. Evaluate figurative and literal relations by their meaning; a cause word, number or genre alone establishes neither outcome.

Establish whether the narrator adopts the expression or reports it as wording from a source. Preserve documented wording that participants inspect, compare, question or correct, even when they examine its accuracy rather than its style. Quotation marks alone establish neither role. Assess ordinary dialogue and adopted narration by the same eligibility-and-function test.

## Development Examples

### Hit

Leah photographs the clay animals before she boxes them, turning each one until its paper name tag shows. Two permission slips still need chasing. She writes herself a reminder, caps the markers and puts the chairs back, **making the classroom quiet in a way that feels forgiving**. Tomorrow she will have to find space for the boxes.

### Hit

At four, even an ordinary search for the wastepaper basket took place inside **the office's secretive hush, the kind offices seem to acquire as the day runs down**. I looked beneath two desks before my colleague pointed to the printer. The cleaner had moved it again. I emptied the pencil shavings and carried on with the accounts.

### Hit

The woman selling peaches pointed uphill. Another flight of steps, then **a square busy with the ease of belonging**: that was where we would find the guesthouse. We dragged our cases across the paving and checked the flowerpots for the promised key. By the time we got the ceiling fan working, the peach stall had closed.

### No Hit

By noon the market was busy enough that we had to walk single file between the stalls. I kept one hand on Sam's backpack so we wouldn't be separated. At the fish counter, I couldn't hear the price over the ice crusher. The seller wrote it on a scrap of cardboard, and I held up two fingers. She wrapped two fillets while Sam counted our remaining coins.

### No Hit

From my room above the workshop, I learned the press operator's routine without ever meeting him. The machine struck four times, paused, then struck four times again. The silences were spaces between words: after a week I could tell when he was changing stock rather than feeding another sheet. On Friday the pause lasted through my whole breakfast. Downstairs, a handwritten sign announced a broken belt.

### No Hit

My mother watched me reach for the shallow tin. Her mouth opened, and I waited for the correction. It closed again. I greased the tin, tipped in the dough and put it somewhere warm. Her silence was a hand withdrawn from the work, though she kept both actual hands firmly round her mug. Only when I asked whether the loaf would overflow did she get up to find the deeper tin.
