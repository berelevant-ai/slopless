# Empty scene state

Input: passage.

Catch thin descriptions of scene elements as empty, quiet, still, cold, heavy or equivalent states, including generic group quieting and silence filling a place. Generalize across unseen wording, inflections and positions.

Preserve observations that locate an actor, establish physical opportunities or obstacles, report relevant conditions, change an inference or contribute a particular narrative relation. Observation, characterization, compression and pacing can themselves be useful; no explicit cause or later action is required.

Judge the candidate state in the complete scene. Short sentences and ordinary adjectives alone do not establish a defect. Concrete details protect their own information while separate stock scene padding remains eligible, including within an otherwise useful contrast.

Assess adopted narration and ordinary dialogue by function. Preserve wording examined or documented as wording. Emotional weather, mood shifts and silence-as-actor may also apply.
