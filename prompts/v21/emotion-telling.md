# Emotion Telling

Read the complete passage, including connected paragraphs. Generalize across unfamiliar wording, inflections and positions. Judge direct emotion reports by their particular contribution in narration, dialogue, first person and nonfiction alike.

Preserve concrete tasks, informative perceptions, consequential social signals and purposeful narrative functions. Observation, orientation, characterization, compression and pacing can supply a function without later action. Context must establish what the candidate label contributes; a named event or causal connector alone does not make a generic reaction informative. Useful information elsewhere protects its own contribution.

Preserve wording examined or documented as wording. A number, quotation mark or genre alone establishes no exemption.

Feelings represented as material can also meet emotion-as-substance; assess each defect independently. An emotion label alone establishes no bodily-reaction defect.
