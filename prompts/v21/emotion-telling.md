# Emotion Telling

Read the complete passage, including connected paragraphs. Generalize across unfamiliar wording, inflections and positions. Judge direct emotion reports by their particular contribution in narration, dialogue, first person and nonfiction alike.

Preserve concrete tasks, informative perceptions, consequential social signals and purposeful narrative functions. Observation, orientation, characterization, compression and pacing can supply a function without later action. Context must establish what the candidate label contributes; a named event or causal connector alone does not make a generic reaction informative. Useful information elsewhere protects its own contribution.

Preserve wording examined or documented as wording. A number, quotation mark or genre alone establishes no exemption.

Feelings represented as material can also meet emotion-as-substance; assess each defect independently. An emotion label alone establishes no bodily-reaction defect.

## Development Examples

### Hit

Dad came to my first swimming final straight from the bakery, with flour still caught in the stitching of his boots. I finished fourth. On the way out, he bought two chocolate milks and asked whether the water had been cold. **I felt deeply disappointed.** We drank beside the vending machine while the next group of swimmers filed past us.

### Hit

Simon, **jealous**, stood at the barbecue with the tongs while his brother explained the junior team's training schedule to two parents. The coaching appointment had been announced that morning. "Room for these?" their father asked, holding out another tray of sausages. Simon shifted the onions to a foil dish. His brother's voice carried easily across the lawn.

### Hit

"I'm afraid I'll miss the scan if I walk to the wrong entrance," Marta told the porter, showing him the letter. He rang the other building and gave her the footpath route behind the pharmacy. The covered walkway on her map had ended at the car park. With the letter folded around the new directions, she set off again. **Marta was upset.**

### No Hit

At the follow-up, the nurse asked whether the new tablets made me sleepy. I said I felt emotionally flat rather than tired: I could stay awake through a film, but a visit from my daughter had meant as little to me as the weather forecast. She wrote down the distinction and asked when it had started. I could date it to the first weekend after the dose increased.

### No Hit

"I'm still angry about you giving him my number," Priya said. "Please don't arrange anything else for me." Her sister put the wedding seating plan aside. "Even if he asks?" "Especially then." They were due downstairs for photographs, but Priya stayed by the door until her sister took out her phone and deleted the message she had begun composing.

Since the accident, I couldn't trust the numb patch under my left foot to tell me when a boot was slipping. It added four miles. At the junction, I moved the sandwiches into an outside pocket and sent Ruth our revised arrival time.
