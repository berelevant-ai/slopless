# Empty Atmosphere Shift

Read the complete passage, including connected paragraphs. Generalize across unfamiliar wording, inflections and positions. Judge subjective assessments as well as asserted changes by whether their particular substance is supplied.

Preserve informative perceptions, concrete conditions, consequential social signals and purposeful narrative functions, including observation, orientation, characterization, compression and pacing. An observed contrast or dramatic change can be informative without producing later action.

Judge the candidate announcement's own contribution. Useful detail elsewhere protects its own meaning; an independent empty announcement can remain. Static scene properties and other defects may overlap with this check.

Assess adopted narration and ordinary dialogue by function; preserve wording examined or documented as wording. A cause word, number, quotation mark or genre alone establishes no exemption.

## Development Examples

### Hit

Heat had gathered beneath the greenhouse glass, enough to make the potting compost dry at the edges of the trays. We watered it before lifting the tomato seedlings. **The place had acquired a curious emotional charge**, and Mum, still wearing the gloves she'd lent me last month, made six spaces on the bench for the larger pots.

### Hit

"**Doesn't feel the same in here any more,**" my cousin said, studying the chip-shop menu.
"Still want peas?"
"Curry sauce. No, peas."
I counted the coins again. By the time we reached the counter he wanted curry sauce after all, and I had to find another coin in my coat.

### Hit

The flash gave me a photograph of my own face instead of the meter reading. I switched it off, held the phone against the cupboard frame, and tried again. In that basement, **which now seemed faintly menacing**, it took three attempts to get all the digits sharp enough for the landlord. I sent the last picture before going upstairs for the laundry.

My daughter learned to step over her instead of calling her to move. At night, a lamp stayed on so she could find her water bowl.
