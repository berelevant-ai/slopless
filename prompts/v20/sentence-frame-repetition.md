# Repeated Openings And Internal Frames

Judge a repeated opening or internal phrase. Set aside recurring endings and duplicated ideas before deciding whether this particular pattern appears.

- TWO immediately adjacent statements qualify when both repeat the same explicit answer, fix, goal, lesson, point, result, trick or equivalent takeaway introduction. Rephrasing the same recommendation after that introduction still counts.
- THREE statements qualify when they repeat an ordinary opening, a distinctive first subject or an internal phrase. A repeated subject can mechanically introduce three different verbs. Internal modal-plus-still wording can qualify despite changed subjects and verbs.
- An internal phrase has further words AFTER it in each statement. A recurring final predicate stays an ending, regardless of how repetitive its meaning feels.

The recurring words must themselves be needless. Preserve comparisons that assign different facts, duties, actions or conditions, instruction organization, useful progression and deliberate refrain. A repeated name, common pronoun or grammatical prefix alone establishes no defect.

A paragraph may contain a useful repeated opening AND bad repeated endings. That remains a no-hit for this check: assess the opening's contribution separately from the endings. Conversely, a separate needless opening remains eligible beside useful facts. Preserve wording documented for examination or correction; assess adopted quotations and ordinary dialogue normally. Adjacent paragraphs can continue one sequence; separate records and topics establish separate sequences.

## Hits

The club will lend its minibus to the food collection team on Wednesdays. **Members can still demonstrate community spirit.** **Volunteers can still show a neighborly attitude.** **Organizers can still display a sense of local fellowship.** Anyone driving must show their license to the secretary before taking the keys, even if they have driven the bus before.

The shared cupboard is moving to the room beside reception next week. **Orderliness makes our workplace better.** **Orderliness improves our working environment.** **Orderliness enhances the place where we work.** Please collect any personal mugs by Friday; unclaimed mugs will be washed and packed with the communal supplies.

We have reduced the number of steps needed to invite an external reviewer. **The result is greater ease.** **The result is improved convenience.** Enter the email address beside the document title and choose an expiry date. Existing reviewers retain their current access until the owner changes it.

## No-Hits

Please leave the shared workbench clear after using the label printer. A little consideration goes a long way. Respect for colleagues goes a long way. Some basic awareness goes a long way. The next shift needs space to lay out the outgoing parcels, and labels left on the cutting mat stick to their envelopes when they stack them.

The menu can still open while offline. The search box can still find saved pages. The editor can still save a local draft. However, sharing requires a connection. Consequently, its button stays disabled offline. Meanwhile, a badge counts pending drafts. Working with confidence is useful. Feeling supported is useful. Having reassurance is useful.

The draft says, "Moreover, for us, effort matters. Furthermore, for us, dedication matters. Ultimately, for us, commitment matters." Remove that sequence; the repeated frame and ending restate one virtue while the connectors pretend to develop it. Start with the replacement date instead. Then give the tracking link and the number customers should call if collection fails.
