# Semantic thinness

Flag a stock claim, metaphor, or narrative beat that substitutes for an identifiable fact, action, observation, or consequence. These existing Slopless examples define the targets and their boundaries.

## Empty conclusions

- Hit: **The answer is consistency.** It leaves the recommended action unstated.
- Hit: **That is the real promise.** It leaves the promise unstated.
- Hit: **The real work begins.** It leaves the work unstated.
- No hit: The fix is to initialize the parser before reading offsets.

## Abstract contrasts and equations

- Hit: **The point is not control. The point is repair.** The contrast supplies labels instead of an operational difference.
- Hit: **The dashboard is the decision.** The equation replaces the relationship it should explain.
- No hit: The bug is not in the parser. It is in the offset mapper.
- No hit: The feed is the product catalog uploaded to S3.

## Unsupported importance

- Hit: The agreement **marks a pivotal turning point for the evolving industry**. The asserted change remains unspecified.
- Hit: **That matters when marketing leaders choose what to do next.** Naming an audience leaves the consequence unstated.
- No hit: The report demonstrates that revenue rose 14 percent after the strategy changed.

## Unspecified change

- Hit: **Everything changed.**
- Hit: **The work became more complicated.**
- Both announce change without identifying its substance or cause.
- No hit: The work became more complicated because the API added pagination.

## Personification and atmosphere

- Hit: The **room seemed to understand** that **something important was beginning to shift**. Both the awareness and change replace observable events.
- Hit: The **silence became heavy**. Weight substitutes for a sound or event.
- No hit: The room sensor reported that occupancy increased after the doors opened.
- No hit: The room was quiet enough to hear the loose hinge scrape.

## Emotion and realization shortcuts

- Hit: **Relief washed over Cassia.** The stock substance metaphor replaces the event or reaction.
- Hit: **She realized the truth.** The discovery remains unstated.
- Hit: **The body knows what it knows.** The wisdom claim supplies no information.
- No hit: Her chest hurt because the harness strap had twisted under her arm.
- No hit: She realized the router was still using the old DNS record.

## Filler action and looking

- Hit: **His eyes moved from the shadows to the rooftops.** The viewing path supplies no finding or search goal.
- Hit: The group **waited quietly**. The beat supplies no purpose or ending condition.
- No hit: She looked under the bench for the missing badge.

## Circular meaning and empty measurement

- Hit: This moment **matters because it invites us to rethink what matters**. The explanation repeats the abstraction.
- Hit: The report **just put a number on it**. The measurement and value remain unstated.
- No hit: The report just put a number on it: 42%.

## Stock wrappers

- Hit: The **solution is boring**: move copy into raw HTML. The instruction leaves the boring-solution wrapper intact.
- No hit: The movie is boring.
- Hit: The **rest of the drill was a blur**. Fog replaces the event sequence.
- No hit: The blur in the photo came from a cracked lens.
- Hit: The courtyard **smelled of disturbed dust and old ash instead of hot metal**. The stacked scent contrast supplies no source.
- No hit: The courtyard smelled of ash because the kiln had cracked during firing.

## Decision boundary

Preserve claims whose context supplies the missing fact, mechanism, observation, or consequence. A name, number, or technical word elsewhere in the paragraph helps only when it explains the targeted claim. Evaluate each claim separately.
