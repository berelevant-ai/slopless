# Superficial-analysis rule

- After reporting an event, the sentence appends a claim of importance, progress, identity, collaboration, or belonging without evidence for that added claim.
- It often starts after a comma with highlighting or underscoring, or after and/which with reflects or underscores.
- Four menu items do not prove the importance of simplicity. A fall from 240 ms to 180 ms does demonstrate lower latency.
- Physical alignment, acoustic resonance, specified receptor activation, and verified durable rows name mechanisms or results.

## Construction

- An event clause followed by a comma or semicolon and a rhetorical participle, such as highlighting, underscoring, showcasing, fostering, confirming, reflecting, or signaling.
- An event followed by and/which and a finite version of those verbs, such as reflects or underscores.
- The added clause claims significance, legacy, inclusion, identity, commitment, or progress without supplying the evidence, mechanism, or measurable consequence.

## Decision

- Flag the unsupported appended claim even when the opening clause names a real event, date, quantity, or organization.
- Preserve an added clause that states a measured result, verified technical property, physical operation, or identified causal mechanism.
- The occurrence of one rhetorical verb or an abstract noun alone does not establish this construction.

#### Desired hits

- The bridge opened in 1998, highlighting the city's commitment to sustainable growth.
- The redesign reduced the menu to four items, underscoring the importance of simplicity.
- The initiative brought the teams together, fostering a culture of meaningful collaboration.
- The conference attracted global speakers, illustrating its growing significance.
- The renewed charter passed unanimously, confirming the institution's lasting significance.
- The foundation funded ten murals, facilitating meaningful cultural engagement.
- The festival ran for three days and reflects the region's enduring cultural significance.
- The bridge opened in the spring, which underscores its lasting legacy.

#### Desired no-hits

- The assay produced a 14 percent increase, confirming receptor activation in sample B.
- The benchmark fell from 240 ms to 180 ms, demonstrating the measured latency reduction.
- The transaction commits after the checksum passes, ensuring that all 14 rows are durable.
- The state machine accepts READY, RUNNING, and FAILED, encompassing all states defined in RFC 9.
- The technician rotated the lens, aligning the sensor with marker B.
- The tuning fork vibrated at 440 Hz, resonating with the open A string.
